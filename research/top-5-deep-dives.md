## OPP-A. AI-native fractional controlling for Belgian SME groups and foreign-owned subsidiaries (consolidation + HQ reporting as the wedge)

1. **Exact problem.** A monthly job nobody inside the customer owns well: (a) 3-15-entity Belgian holdings must produce group numbers (consolidated P&L/BS/cash flow with intercompany eliminated) for banks, boards, family councils or buyers, but are legally exempt from statutory consolidation (group of limited scope: not more than one of EUR 42.5M turnover, EUR 21.25M balance sheet, 250 FTE, from FY2024) so they never bought tooling or hired a specialist; it is done in Excel by the owner-CFO or once a year by the accountant. (b) Belgian/Luxembourg subsidiaries of foreign groups must deliver an HQ-format monthly pack (group chart of accounts, GAAP adjustments, IC confirmations, variance commentary, KPI schedules) on HQ's close calendar from a local ledger with a 1-3 person team that breaks in peaks and turnover.
2. **Target customer.** (a) Owner-CEO or family CFO of a Belgian holding with 3-15 entities, group revenue EUR 5-60M (ESTIMATE), on Odoo 18/19, Exact Online or Yuki, without a group controller. (b) Local finance manager or managing director of a 20-250 FTE Belgian/Luxembourg subsidiary reporting to a foreign HQ; budget line "interim finance / professional fees"; HQ CFO nod often needed.
3. **Evidence of demand.** Odoo removed its Consolidation app in v18 and marks the replacement deprecated in the v19 docs; five forum threads (Mar-May 2026) ask how to do eliminations; Odoo 19.2 changed multi-ledger journal handling. Group Controller average salary EUR 64,900 (StepStone; ~EUR 90-100k employer cost, ESTIMATE); live "Financial Controller (Consolidation)" vacancies at EUR 2,500-4,500/month in West Flanders family groups. Interim controllers EUR 600-800/day, interim CFO EUR 900-1,400/day, rates rising under the Dosign equal-pay ruling (1 Jan 2026). PwC Belgium sells consolidation-as-a-service to SMEs; Vandelanotte and VGD market consolidation teams; FR/LU boutiques (MELVAN, Primexis, BM&A, Fiducia Management) sell outsourced consolidation. Population: ~5,500 groups in the Statbel/FPB enterprise-group database (2015 basis), 30,552 holdings (commercial database), 80,210 management companies (2024, doubled since 2019), 7,288 foreign-controlled firms in Flanders alone (Belgium ESTIMATE 11-13k). Not evidenced: any Belgian SME group publicly paying for *monthly management* consolidation; all outsourced-consolidation prices found are statutory/annual (HYPOTHESIS to test).
4. **Existing alternatives.** Owner's Excel; accountant once a year; hiring a medior controller; interim controller days; Big-4 managed services; NL SaaS run by the client's own staff.
5. **Direct competitors.** PwC CaaS, EY FAAS, KPMG managed services (quote-only, INFERENCE >EUR 5k/month); Vandelanotte, VGD, Crowe, BDO/Moore-type firms (statutory, annual mindset, capacity-constrained: 56% of firms refuse clients); interim/fractional supply (Apex League, Michael Page, Robert Half, Finvision, FinForces, Flow Partners) at EUR 600-1,400/day; Easyclose (NL, EUR 705-905/month for 10 entities), Finstack (from EUR 39/entity/month), Speedbooks (EUR 69/month), Liquid, Syft (USD 399/month unlimited), Fathom; BrightAnalytics and EMAsphere (Belgian, quote-only, Odoo/Exact connectors); Exact Consolidatie; Silverfin (firm channel); Lucanet/Prophix-Sigma Conso/Tagetik (enterprise).
6. **Indirect competitors.** Microsoft Copilot Finance Agents (reconciliation agent GA April 2026, variance-analysis agent, Excel Skills June 2026; native to D365/SAP, not Odoo/Exact per release notes, INFERENCE); Odoo 19 AI agents; Neno (NL, EUR 6.6M, Belgium H2 2027) and Skalar (DE, EUR 12M) at the bookkeeping/tax layer; Peliqan/Chift connectors.
7. **Pricing.** Corridor derived from verified anchors: software floor EUR 400-900/month plus the client's labour; two controller days EUR 1,200-1,600/month; employed controller EUR 7.5-8.5k/month. Proposed: EUR 1,500-3,500/month (a), EUR 2,000-4,000/month (b), onboarding EUR 2,500-7,500 (ESTIMATE).
8. **Market size.** Belgium TAM (a) ~4,000 groups x EUR 24k = ~EUR 96M/yr; (b) ~3,600 subsidiaries x EUR 30k = ~EUR 108M/yr (ESTIMATE on HYPOTHESIS filters). SAM ~EUR 60M/yr; SOM 10-18 clients = EUR 250-450k/yr, capped by founder hours, not market. Benelux TAM ~EUR 550-650M/yr (ESTIMATE). EU order of magnitude EUR 2-3bn/yr (ESTIMATE, product phase only).
9. **Market growth.** Positive: holding/management-company formation doubling 2019-2024; PE/holding roll-ups; Odoo growth; shortage profession; interim rates rising; capital flowing to agent-first finance services.
10. **Why now.** Ledger access became cheap (Odoo App Store MCP modules, OSS Odoo MCP with 384 stars, Peliqan/Chift); LLM trial-balance mapping is studied and works; Odoo's live consolidation gap; labour alternative dearer and scarcer; 2024 threshold uplift widened the exempt population.
11. **Why customers switch.** Trigger events: bank covenant reporting, new entity/acquisition, sale preparation, controller resignation, Odoo migration, HQ format change, audit findings on IC. Without a trigger, segment (a) does not switch (HYPOTHESIS).
12. **Why incumbents have not solved it.** Software vendors assume a controller exists on the client side; firms are organised around the annual statutory cycle and are at capacity; Big-4 and interim agencies are people-priced and would cannibalise themselves; Odoo treats small-group consolidation as a niche.
13. **Competitive wedge.** The empty cell: monthly, done-for-you, Odoo/Exact/Yuki-native management consolidation plus HQ pack for sub-EUR-60M groups and subsidiaries at EUR 1.5-4k/month, delivered by an ex-corporate controller with agent tooling. Nobody in Belgium surfaced in that cell.
14. **Barrier-to-entry analysis.** Capital EUR 500-2,000 (registration ~EUR 190, liability insurance ~EUR 270/yr, LLM credits, optional connector subscription). Technical: founder can build extraction, mapping, IC matching, pack rendering and QA in ~170-240 h (4-6 months at 10 h/week); an engineer is needed only for a multi-tenant product. Regulatory: decision-critical (item 15). Distribution: relationship-driven (medium). Sales cycle 1-3 months (a) with a trigger, 2-6 months (b). Operational: close-week collision with the founder's own FP&A close (HIGH for this founder specifically). Data: each onboarding builds a mapping/IC library (switching cost, GOOD barrier). Partnership: Odoo integrators and ITAA firms want references first. Trust: high for an unknown; credentials, insurance, QA process, references lower it. Network effects: none in service, weak in product. Overall: entry LOW-MEDIUM, scale MEDIUM-HIGH; regulatory and trust barriers become GOOD barriers if an ITAA partnership is in place; the close-week barrier is a BAD barrier.
15. **Regulatory analysis.** Law of 17 March 2019, art. 3, reserves to ITAA members (self-employed, for third parties): organisation of accounting services; opening, keeping, centralising and closing of books; drawing up annual accounts; verification and correction of accounting documents; and "analysis, by means of accounting techniques, of the situation and functioning of undertakings from the point of view of their credit, performance and risks". Illegal practice is a criminal offence (art. 117); ITAA runs a hotline and wins cases (Kortrijk, Dec 2024: suspended prison and fine; end-2025 request to prosecutors to prioritise). Financial advice, financial reporting and assisting companies financially are not reserved (ITAA service-desk and sector glossaries). Court of Cassation, 28 Nov 2022: a subcontractor executing part of an ITAA firm's engagement letter is not acting "for third parties" and need not be registered; ITAA has a sous-traitance page. Application (INFERENCE, needs written confirmation): management consolidation, HQ packs, commentary and IC matching lists delivered to the client's own bookkeepers are most likely open; posting eliminations, "cleaning up" ledgers and statutory consolidated accounts are likely reserved. Safe structures: contract as controlling/reporting with explicit exclusion of bookkeeping and statutory accounts, client's bookkeeper posts proposed entries; subcontract to an ITAA firm for anything ledger-touching. GDPR: founder acts as processor; art. 28 DPA, EU hosting, zero-retention LLM terms, account-level aggregates only. Professional liability EUR 270-1,000/yr. Founder legal form: sole proprietor under the VAT franchise (<EUR 25k) first, BV/SRL around EUR 80k turnover.
16. **Belgium strategy.** Advantages: density of holdings and foreign subsidiaries, Odoo is Belgian and its gap is live, controller shortage, firms at capacity (channel), Brussels HQ concentration. Disadvantages: ITAA reserved activities are stricter than NL/UK; Flemish family groups (where the consolidation vacancies are) expect Dutch; employment loyalty duties. Start with Brussels/Walloon-Brabant groups and English-working foreign subsidiaries; add Dutch or a Flemish partner later. Client-side subsidies: Wallonia digital-maturity vouchers may cover a "reporting automation" onboarding; Flanders consulting subsidy now cyber-only; Brussels consultancy subsidy suspended since 12 Aug 2026.
17. **EU expansion.** Luxembourg first (French, Brussels-adjacent, subsidiaries and SOPARFIs; entry as subcontractor to fiduciaries or HQ-pack producer; Lux GAAP, OEC-regulated); then France (Odoo-heavy, outsourced consolidation is an established category, expert-comptable monopoly on bookkeeping but management reporting open); NL only with a Dutch-speaking partner (most crowded tool market); UK/DE only as product.
18. **Founder fit 10/10.** It is the founder's own job done for several companies; the asymmetric advantage is corporate-group reporting know-how (HQ formats, IC discipline, GAAP bridges) plus the ability to build the agent tooling. A generic founder without controlling experience cannot credibly sell accountability for group numbers.
19. **Interest fit 8/10.** Finance modelling, data, AI agents, B2B customer conversations with finance peers, strategy of building a product from a service. Downsides: repetitive close work, deadline stress on top of a day job.
20. **AI architecture.** Read-only connectors (Odoo JSON-2/XML-RPC via OSS/App Store MCP; Exact REST with OAuth and 5,000 requests/day cap; Yuki API; CSV drop folder for no-API entities; optional Peliqan/Chift). Postgres/DuckDB schema per client: entity, period, local and group accounts, versioned mapping, TB lines, IC pairs, elimination entries, FX, pack templates, commentary, QA checks. LLM tasks: mapping proposals with confidence, IC exception classification and email drafting, commentary from computed bridges, pack tie-out reading. Deterministic: FX, aggregation, IC matching rules, eliminations, bridges, rendering (openpyxl/python-pptx), QA rules, audit log. Human-in-the-loop: founder approves mappings and eliminations and signs; client posts entries.
21. **AI leverage.** 40-60% of monthly hours executable by agents/software today; 75-90% in three years (ESTIMATE). AI makes the business cheaper, faster, more scalable per founder-hour; it does not make it more defensible.
22. **AI commoditisation scenario.** Tool layer already commoditised (Finstack EUR 39/entity, Syft/Fathom AI commentary, Copilot variance agent, Odoo AI): software value WEAKENED. Service layer: NEUTRAL (commoditisation lowers the founder's delivery cost as much as competitors'); margins STRENGTHENED in the service phase.
23. **AGI scenario.** DESTROYED for labour revenue; WEAKENED rather than destroyed only if the founder holds an accountable, insured, ITAA-partnered signatory position for a client base by then (HYPOTHESIS). Window: 3-7 years.
24. **ASI scenario.** DESTROYED as a knowledge service; residual is legal accountability, which regulation could also reassign.
25. **Business model.** Fixed monthly retainer per group/subsidiary plus onboarding fee; alternatives: per-entity (EUR 250-400/entity/month with EUR 1,500 floor), onboarding-then-hand-over with light QA retainer (EUR 500-900/month), close-week package for subsidiaries.
26. **Unit economics.** Steady-state 4-6 h/client/month with tooling (10-14 h in the first three months); EUR 2,000/month at 5 h = EUR 400/h effective; direct costs EUR 100-240/client/month; gross margin 85-92% (ESTIMATE). Capacity 6-10 clients at 10-15 h/week; ceiling ~EUR 18k/month (EUR ~215k/yr). Onboarding (25-40 h) is where fixed-fee risk sits.
27. **Customer acquisition strategy.** Warm network first; then Odoo integrators in Brussels/Wallonia (white-label consolidation onboarding), small ITAA firms with client stops (referral or Cassation-route subcontracting), LinkedIn content in EN/FR on Odoo 19 consolidation and IC checklists, Luxembourg fiduciaries; later Odoo partner events, EMAsphere/BrightAnalytics implementation partnership, bank relationship managers, Big-4 alumni and HQ-controller communities.
28. **First 10 customers.** #1: an ex-colleague now finance manager of a Brussels subsidiary, or a family group known via the employer's ecosystem, on a 3-month pilot at EUR 1,000-1,500/month with free onboarding for a reference. #2-10: two or three Odoo integrators, two or three ITAA firms with client stops, LinkedIn inbound from Odoo-gap content, one Luxembourg fiduciary. Objections and answers: "our accountant does it" (annually; we do monthly and hand them a clean IC position); "HQ will not open the ERP" (exports, read-only, DPA/NDA, EU-hosted); "one-person risk" (documented process, backup freelancer, tooling the client keeps); "liability" (insurance; we propose, you post; statutory stays with ITAA/auditor).
29. **MVP.** One client served by hand: exports, Excel mapping, IC matching script, pack in the client's template, LLM-drafted commentary, review call. Automate in order: TB ingestion and validation; mapping table with LLM proposals and versions; IC matching and open-item lists; pack rendering from the DB; QA tie-out rules; commentary drafting.
30. **What can be done manually.** Everything in year 1; the first two months of each client are manual by design.
31. **What should be automated.** Extraction, mapping proposals, IC first pass, bridge computation, rendering, QA. Do NOT build in year 1: multi-tenant SaaS, an Odoo module, statutory consolidation, IFRS engines, budgeting, dashboards, connectors without a client, anything that posts into client ledgers.
32. **30-day validation plan.** Week 1: check the employment contract and work rules for side-activity and loyalty clauses; request a written ITAA/legal read on art. 3 scope for management reporting and IC analysis (EUR 300-600 for a lawyer's letter, ESTIMATE); register as complementary self-employed only once a paid pilot is likely. Weeks 1-3: 8-10 interviews per segment (questions in `_raw/deep-OPP-A.md` section 17), half Flemish for segment (a), plus 3 Odoo integrators, 3 ITAA firms, 1 Luxembourg fiduciary. Week 3-4: offer one pilot at EUR 1,000-1,500/month with free onboarding; deliver the first pack manually. Success = 1 signed pilot and at least 3 interviewees confirming monthly cadence and a budget line.
33. **90-day plan.** Months 1-2: pilot #1 delivered twice; mapping and IC library started; DPA and engagement template in place. Month 2-3: pilot #2 (other segment); publish two LinkedIn pieces; ask one Odoo integrator for a referral arrangement; build TB ingestion and IC matching scripts; decide on ITAA-firm subcontracting partner. Success = 2 paying clients, hours per pack falling, no missed deadline.
34. **EUR 1k/month path.** One segment-(a) client at EUR 1,000-1,500 done largely manually; 3-6 months via network.
35. **EUR 10k/month path.** 4-6 clients with the tooling of item 20; 12-24 months; requires clients with WD10+ deadlines and a decision on employment (4/5 or leave).
36. **EUR 100k/month path.** Not reachable solo. Either a 6-10 person agent-first controlling firm (needs capital and ITAA partnership) or product revenue (~400 groups at EUR 250/month or 100 firms/integrators at EUR 1k/month) if the Odoo/Exact-native product finds a channel and Odoo does not close the gap.
37. **Main risks.** Close-week collision with employment (highest, operational); ITAA scope (regulatory); unproven monthly-management WTP in segment (a); Odoo platform risk; employer conflict; Flemish language; solo ceiling ~EUR 200k/yr; commoditisation of "the numbers"; key-person/trust; HQ security blocking API access.
38. **Reasons NOT to pursue.** If the founder's goal is a venture-scale software company, OPP-A is a funding engine and a learning vehicle, not the destination; its AGI/ASI classification is DESTROYED for the labour part; the operational barrier hurts this founder more than any competitor.
39. **What evidence would change the recommendation.** Toward strong pursuit: written ITAA/legal confirmation of scope; two paying pilots from the network within 90 days; an Odoo integrator agreeing to refer; three subsidiary finance managers confirming HQ packs are outsourced or interim-staffed today at >=EUR 2k/month. Toward drop: reserved-activity confirmation with no firm willing to partner; employer prohibition; Odoo 20 shipping full consolidation with IC eliminations; interviews showing quarterly/annual cadence is all that is wanted.

**Trajectory.** Year 1: 2-4 clients, EUR 3-8k/month by month 12, tooling built around real clients, ITAA partnership signed, decision on employment. Year 2: 6-10 clients, EUR 12-20k/month at the hour ceiling, fixed-fee onboarding, first white-label deliveries through integrators, one freelance controller for close weeks. Year 3: web app with Odoo/Exact/Yuki connectors for own clients and 3-5 firms/integrators at EUR 250-400/entity/month or EUR 1k/firm; service still >70% of revenue. Year 5 (conditional): 100-300 groups on the product plus a small controlling team, EUR 1-3M/yr (HYPOTHESIS); otherwise a EUR 200-400k/yr specialist service or a sale to a firm/integrator.

**Regret test.** If in 10 years a Benelux "agent-first controlling firm with an Odoo-native consolidation product" is a EUR 20-50M business, the founder would regret not testing it, because he is one of the few people with both the controlling and the tooling skills and the Odoo gap was live in 2026.

MANAGEABLE: employment for segment (a) (must inform the employer; serving a competitor is serious cause), churn (18-24 months, weak data), GDPR. Mitigations the red team found: no prosecutions of controllers surfaced; ITAA's June 2026 statement frames reserved scope as bookkeeping and annual accounts; a seven-year-experience exemption allows sitting the certified-accountant exam directly. Contradictions logged: the red team could not find the "Dosign" equal-pay ruling the deep dive cited (treat as unverified); it assumed the founder's employer is a foreign-owned subsidiary, which is unknown. ## OPP-B. Peppol portfolio operations for Belgian accounting firms (exception triage, master-data hygiene, then 2028 e-reporting reconciliation)

1. **Exact problem.** Since 1 Jan 2026 Belgian B2B invoices must be structured e-invoices over Peppol; wrong VAT numbers, wrong scheme (0208 vs 9925), duplicate identifiers (1.6M Peppol IDs for ~946k companies), missing fields and unregistered recipients cause rejections and undelivered invoices; fines EUR 1,500/3,000/5,000 apply since April 2026; accounting firms' clients sit on many access points so nobody sees the whole portfolio. From 1 Jan 2028, dual-sided near-real-time e-reporting will expose mismatches between what FPS Finance sees and the ledger.
2. **Target customer.** Belgian accounting firms with 5-50 staff (three quarters of firms have <=10 staff; firm count unverified, 5-7k inferred), cloud stacks (Exact/Yuki/Octopus/Adsolut, concentrated in Flanders), whose partners own client data quality.
3. **Evidence of demand.** ITAA (July 2026) frames the post-mandate job as the accountant's shift "to advice on data quality"; AccountancyVandaag/Unizo pain points are "where is my invoice", workload (+33% of bookkeepers in a Clearfacts webinar), slower payment of Peppol invoices; 1.06M businesses (89% of VAT-liable) registered. Weak: the Belgian Peppol Service Providers Forum observed ~1% problematic exchanges post-launch and expects improvement; no fine enforcement statistics or named cases found; rejection triage never appears as a top-3 practitioner pain.
4. **Existing alternatives.** Free bulk checkers (e-invoice.be Peppol Radar CSV, peppolcheck.be, Peppol Practical, Nymus, Finplex); free Peppol Directory REST API (2 queries/s) and full recipient list; KBO open data; vendor support tickets; Excel.
5. **Direct competitors.** Portfolio status views already inside OkiOki (three-badge Peppol status per client), Billit's free accountant portal (badges, audit logs, per-invoice evidence files), Yuki (domain UBL overview, "not delivered" notifications, activity log); Peliqan's Billit MCP persists acknowledgements and pitches "agent flags a rejected invoice and triggers a resend" at EUR 150/month, with Exact/Yuki/Silverfin/Odoo connectors and a pan-EU Peppol MCP planned.
6. **Indirect competitors.** Access points (Banqup 85k SMEs, Peppol Box EUR 5-7/month), Cashfeed (AP agent, 350 clients), Codabox VOILA, ERP integrators bundling clean-up, advisory content (Taxilla, b2btax, VATupdate).
7. **Pricing.** Anchors: EUR 0-10/SME/month (access points), EUR 100-300/firm/month (Peliqan EUR 150, Clearnox EUR 100, AdminPulse EUR 290). Proposed: health check EUR 900-2,500 per firm; monitoring EUR 75-250/firm/month; 2028 readiness audit EUR 1,500-3,000 (ESTIMATE).
8. **Market size.** Belgian SAM ~EUR 1-5M/yr; solo SOM EUR 35-250k/yr (ESTIMATE). EUR 100k/month not reachable firm-by-firm in Belgium.
9. **Market growth.** Phase-1 pain expected to decline through 2026-27; step change only on 1 Jan 2028 (e-reporting) and with Luxembourg's 2028 receipt mandate; ViDA 2030.
10. **Why now.** Mandate 8 months old; identifier mess real; 2028 law approved July 2026 (pre-draft 18 Jul; law due autumn 2026; dataset only in an early-2027 Royal Decree).
11. **Why customers switch.** Only if hours lost and liability documentation are quantified; otherwise they wait for their vendor.
12. **Why incumbents have not solved it.** Each vendor sees only its own accounts; cross-vendor is nobody's product; but Peliqan is closest and the problem is shrinking.
13. **Competitive wedge.** Cross-access-point, per-client exceptions and identifier hygiene for the firm; timing wedge into 2028 reconciliation. Thin.
14. **Barrier-to-entry analysis.** Capital <EUR 1k. Technical: deterministic lookups plus OSS MCP servers; replicable in weeks. Regulatory: "checking and correcting bookkeeping documents" is ITAA-reserved, so the service must stay at the identifier/transport layer; no access-point certification needed to read APIs; GDPR art. 28 DPA. Data access: vendor API terms and per-firm consent (partner programmes at Billit/Exact). Distribution: Flemish cloud firms (Dutch) vs Brussels/Wallonia on-prem stacks (Horus/WinBooks/BOB, no APIs). Overall LOW and BAD (nothing accumulates except relationships).
15. **Regulatory analysis.** As above; plus Peppol Directory and KBO licence terms for bulk queries (to check).
16. **Belgium strategy.** Belgium is the first big-bang mandate: a live laboratory. Brussels/Wallonia firms are the founder's language fit but the wrong stack; Flanders is the right stack but needs Dutch.
17. **EU expansion.** Luxembourg only (Peppol four-corner, draft law 17 Jul 2026, receipt 1 Jan 2028, ~555 OEC firms); France needs a re-platform (PA/annuaire model); Germany has no network; NL no mandate until ~2030.
18. **Founder fit 7/10.** Python, data-quality and process skills fit; no accounting-firm network; no Dutch.
19. **Interest fit 7/10.** Compliance plumbing is less interesting than group finance; the 2028 reconciliation engine is the interesting part.
20. **AI architecture.** Deterministic identifier normalisation, directory/KBO/VIES checks, reason taxonomy; LLM for rejection-reason classification and NL/FR fix-list emails; connectors to Billit/Yuki/Exact via OSS MCP; later reconciliation engine against the 2028 dataset.
21. **AI leverage.** Low-moderate; mostly software, not AI.
22. **AI commoditisation scenario.** WEAKENED: MCP servers and vendor agents already exist.
23. **AGI scenario.** NEUTRAL-to-WEAKENED: compliance data work persists but is done by the ledger vendor's agent.
24. **ASI scenario.** DESTROYED.
25. **Business model.** One-off audits, per-firm monitoring subscription, 2027 readiness audits, 2028 reconciliation module; OEM to Codabox/Billit/Peliqan as the realistic exit.
26. **Unit economics.** Health check 15-30 h at EUR 900-2,500 = EUR 60-100/h; monitoring EUR 75-250/firm/month; margins high, volumes small.

## OPP-C. Productised QoE-light financial analysis for small Belgian acquisitions

1. **Exact problem.** Below EUR 5-10M enterprise value, financial due diligence is skipped or done ad hoc by the buyer's accountant; buyers overpay, miss working-capital and normalisation issues, and banks get weak credit files.
2. **Target customer.** Buyers of EUR 0.5-5M businesses: individual acquirers and successors (~20,000 businesses/yr face succession; ~185,000 self-employed aged 55+; external sales now exceed intra-family), small PE and corporates buying tuck-ins, Belgian search funds (single digits/yr since 2021); advised by brokers, Wallonie Entreprendre-labelled transmission partners, accountants and notaries.
3. **Evidence of demand.** Vlerick 2026: small-deal rebound, retirement the primary sale driver, 3.8-6.1 bidders per <EUR 5M target; Wallonia accompanied transmissions 428 (2023) to 519 (2024); Wallonia Cheque-Transmission covers 75% of DD fees up to EUR 15k (labelled providers only). Price ladders: NL external DD EUR 2-20k, FR forfaits EUR 10-25k, UK GBP 5-15k, US from USD 8,900 (Rapid Diligence), CH CHF 8-60k. No evidence banks require an independent DD report.
4. **Existing alternatives.** Buyer's accountant "has a look"; lawyer checklists; broker's light DD; skip.
5. **Direct competitors.** Analyzediz (Flanders, "independent, affordable" small-deal DD, two-week target); Syno M&A pre-audit (7-day legal audit, verified EBITDA); Bol Adviseurs "verkort boekenonderzoek" (NL, April 2026); mid-tier firms (Baker Tilly, Moore, VGD, BDO, Vandelanotte) bespoke.
6. **Indirect competitors.** AI QoE tooling for advisers (Finsider, Keye, Termina, Hebbia, DiligentIQ, QoEAgent); Companyweb/Altares data; brokers.
7. **Pricing.** Fixed-fee ladder EUR 3,500 / 5,500 / 8,500 by EV band (ESTIMATE); accountant hours EUR 80-150/h as the substitute price.
8. **Market size.** 1,500-2,500 deals/yr at EUR 0.5-5M EV (ESTIMATE); TAM ~EUR 11M; SAM EUR 2.8-3.9M (FR-reachable ~EUR 1-1.5M); part-time SOM 15-25 reports/yr = EUR 80-140k gross, EUR 50-90k net of a signatory share.

10. **Why now.** AI cuts analyst hours on ledger ingestion, chart-of-accounts mapping and drafting; succession volumes rising; regional vouchers.
11. **Why customers switch.** Net cost after voucher; two-week promise; bank-ready pack; independence from the seller's adviser.
12. **Why incumbents have not solved it.** Mid-tier hourly, partner-led models are uneconomic at EUR 5k; US productisation came with the searcher wave; Belgian boutiques (Analyzediz, Syno) are new and Flemish.
13. **Competitive wedge.** Fixed price, two weeks, FR-speaking Brussels/Wallonia, voucher-eligible, sold via accountants at capacity and brokers; co-branded with an ITAA/IBR signatory.
14. **Barrier-to-entry analysis.** Capital EUR 0-2k (templates, scripts, LLM, liability insurance EUR 300-800/yr). Technical: NBB XBRL/JSON parsing (free NBB Consult; webservice EUR 12,400/yr; Improved Data EUR 3,300/yr), bank-statement and ledger normalisation. Regulatory: decisive (item 15). Distribution: adviser-mediated; Wallonie Entreprendre labelling needs 3 years' documented experience. Sales cycle: deal-driven, two-week SLA collides with a day job. Trust: signature and references. Overall: LOW to enter, HIGH to practise legally solo; the regulatory barrier is GOOD only for licensed incumbents.
15. **Regulatory analysis.** Art. 3 of the law of 17 March 2019 reserves 4° verification and correction of accounting documents and 5° "analysis by accounting techniques of the situation and functioning of enterprises from the viewpoint of creditworthiness, profitability and risks" to ITAA-registered accountants when done self-employed for third parties; ITAA prosecutes (three court wins 2022; Kortrijk Dec 2024: six months suspended prison, fine, damages; end-2025 request to all prosecutors). A non-ITAA founder selling target financial analysis in his own name is exposed. Routes: an ITAA/IBR signatory partner (30-40% share, ESTIMATE); employment or subcontracting inside a firm (Cassation 2022 route); selling tooling to advisers. A lawyer's opinion is open question #1.
16. **Belgium strategy.** Brussels/Wallonia via Cheque-Transmission-labelled partners, hub.brussels 1819, Semaine de la Transmission; Flanders is the largest, highest-multiple market (5.1x vs 4.3x Brussels) but Dutch and already served by Analyzediz.
17. **EU expansion.** NL (mid-tier already productised), FR (forfaits exist), LU (regulated), UK (mature); only as tooling.
18. **Founder fit 9/10** on skills (normalised EBITDA, working capital, cash conversion); 4/10 on licence.

21. **AI leverage.** HIGH on ingestion, mapping and drafting (19-24 h per report with tooling vs 28-35 manual).

23. **AGI scenario.** FRAGILE/WEAKENED: analysis becomes free; signature and liability remain with licensed professionals.
24. **ASI scenario.** DESTROYED.

26. **Unit economics.** EUR 5,500 / 22 h = EUR 250/h before signatory share; EUR 150-175/h after; capacity 1-2 reports/month part-time; margin >85% on direct costs.

## OPP-D. Brussels co-ownership energy-renovation financing packs
