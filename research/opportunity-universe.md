# Opportunity universe

Date: 2026-09-11. Method: five parallel discovery streams (A finance workflows, B regulation, C Belgian verticals, D AI-adoption gap and startup landscape, E customer-pain mining) produced 130 raw entries; merged and deduplicated here into 102 unique opportunities. Raw stream files with per-item evidence URLs are in `_raw/`. Evidence labels: everything in this programme comes from WebSearch result summaries (pages could not be opened), so "VERIFIED" always means VERIFIED (search-summary).

Founder assumptions applied at screening: Brussels-based, employed full time, ~10-15 h/week, limited capital, corporate-finance/controlling background, learning Python/SQL/AI agents, English + working French assumed, Dutch flagged where material.

Screening rule (universe -> 40 investigated): keep if (a) pain is recurring and evidenced, (b) some willingness-to-pay signal exists or is plausible, (c) founder can reach the buyer, (d) the opportunity is not already commoditised by incumbents, (e) it fits a finance/operations/data founder. Everything dropped is recorded in `rejected-opportunities.md` with the reason category.
