## 2. Kill hypotheses — evidence, verdict, mitigation

### H1. Sunhat / EcoVadis extend into exactly this → redundant
- Sunhat: no public pricing, no SME/self-serve tier, no Benelux presence found; positions as "AI-Software for Enterprise Teams"; German Mittelstand suppliers first; is an official EcoVadis Consulting Partner and runs a *partner programme for ESG consultancies and auditors* (VERIFIED, Q01/Q02/Q24/Q35). Sunhat covers ESG only; no NIS2/CyFun.
- EcoVadis: (a) 2026 methodology credits VSME-aligned reports (Basic 75/100, Basic+Comprehensive 100/100) for companies <1,000 FTE (VERIFIED, Q13); (b) **EcoVadis Vitals** = free light questionnaire for small suppliers unable to do full Ratings (VERIFIED, Q22).
- Verdict: **MANAGEABLE for the answering-service phase** (Sunhat will not do Dutch/French evidence prep for an 80-FTE plant; it might even be the founder's tool as a partner); **SERIOUS for the SaaS/passport phase** (EcoVadis already owns the buyer-accepted ESG passport and now has a free SME on-ramp; VSME gets credit inside EcoVadis, so the "ESG library" collapses into "one VSME report").
- Mitigation: do not build ESG software; position as Kube/EcoVadis/Sunhat-adjacent service; ESG pillar = VSME Basic once, re-used.

### H2. Buyers refuse third-party passport; insist on own portals
- UCB: suppliers expected to reach >=45/100 per EcoVadis theme (VERIFIED, Q04). Port of Antwerp-Bruges: CyFun Basic (verified) or ISO 27001 for API-connected logistics subcontractors (VERIFIED, Q08). Colruyt, Delhaize, Bekaert, Solvay, Umicore supplier-questionnaire policies: Evidence not found (Q03/Q07/Q40). UNIZO: 41% of food SMEs juggle 3-5 systems, 19% >6 (VERIFIED, Q42) — proof that buyers impose their own portals.
- Kube ESG (Isabel/Febelfin/4 banks) is explicitly a "share once with banks, customers, financiers" passport; no corporate buyer publicly accepting it found (Q25/Q31).
- Verdict: **SERIOUS for the two-sided/passport variant; IRRELEVANT for the service phase** (the thesis already answers "in each customer's format"). The buyer-accepted passports that exist are labels (EcoVadis medal, CyFun label, ISO 27001), not evidence packs; a founder-run passport has no chance of displacing them.
- Mitigation: drop the two-sided ambition; treat portals as the delivery surface (re-keying is part of the service).

### H3. Value-chain cap removes the pain
- Cap = VSME Basic (11 disclosure requirements) for CSRD reporters' requests to <1,000-FTE suppliers, FY from 1 Jan 2027 (VERIFIED, Q05). EcoVadis credits VSME (Q13). Kube ESG: free VSME platform with AI, free for <=250-FTE SMEs through 2026, ~100 data points/40 mandatory (VERIFIED, Q14/Q19). Workable Method: free for Fenavian members, >20 federations, designed so SMEs "fill in questionnaires themselves, without expensive consultants" (VERIFIED, Q33). Sustainability advice no longer subsidised in Flanders since 1 Feb 2026 (VERIFIED, Q18).
- Counter-evidence: the cap binds CSRD reporters only; retailers/banks using EcoVadis/Sedex/own portals still generate re-keying work (INFERENCE); UNIZO burden is portal multiplicity, not content.
- Verdict: **SERIOUS for pricing the ESG pillar** — content is capped by law and served free by banks and federations; only re-keying and evidence hygiene remain, worth EUR 0-100/month, not EUR 150-500.
- Mitigation: price ESG as an add-on to a security-led service, or as per-portal re-keying.

### H4. Security-attestation liability without credentials
- PI insurance for consultants (incorrect advice causing financial loss) available from Hiscox, Baloise, Allianz, Vivium, AG, Axa; no NIS2-specific exclusion found (VERIFIED, Q09; premium ENF). A completed questionnaire referenced in a contract is a legal representation; many questionnaires say false statements = breach/disqualification; misrepresentation is a leading cause of cyber-claim denial (VERIFIED, Q27). CCB: self-assessment is the company's own; only a BELAC-accredited, CCB-authorised CAB can verify and only CCB issues the label (VERIFIED, Q06/Q26). DE/AT generalist consultancies openly sell "we formulate the answers with you, honestly" (Q36/Q38).
- Verdict: **MANAGEABLE** — the deep dive slightly over-killed. The service drafts; the CEO signs; the MSP owns technical facts. The unresolved item is whether an insurer will write PI for an uncredentialed part-timer at an affordable premium (Evidence not found).
- Mitigation: engagement letter "drafting support, no attestation"; MSP countersigns technical answers; PI quote before first client; never answer "verified" questions (Port of Antwerp requires a CAB label anyway).

### H5. Accountants and MSPs will not be a channel
- Wolters Kluwer/ITAA survey (>600 firms): >56% refuse new clients; 46% do not want to grow; **58% rank workload reduction above investing in digital tools or training** (VERIFIED, Q11). Accounting firms market VSME support (Vandelanotte, VGD, PKF BOFIDI) but nobody sells questionnaire answering (Q10). Eevery, Kube ESG (via Sustenuto webinars) and Karomia already court accountants for VSME.
- MSPs: ECP white-label CyFun platform EUR 25-9,075/client/month (Q15); ITAF etc. position on NIS2; NL MSP-adjacent services (InstantSecure, SecuroNova EUR 399/mo) already sell "security evidence for SME suppliers" (Q32/Q37). Kmo-portefeuille subsidises only *registered* cybersecurity-advice providers (45%/35%) — registration/quality label needed (Q18).
- Verdict: **SERIOUS**. Accountants are capacity-constrained and already have free/near-free VSME channels (Kube, Eevery); MSPs already have a CyFun evidence platform and will keep security answers in-house. The founder would be a fourth mouth at the SME's table.
- Mitigation: sell to the MSP as white-label labour (answer drafting per questionnaire), not to accountants; or become the ITAA-facing "Kube-ready" data-prep resource.

### H6. Founder fit (no security background, no Dutch, no supplier network)
- 43% of surveyed Flemish firms use Dutch as sole official language; others NL+EN or NL+FR (VERIFIED but old 2015 thesis, Q30). Kube, VLAIO, CCB, ITAA materials are NL/FR; the manufacturing/logistics/port cluster is Flemish. Wallonia chèques (cyber up to 75%) and hub.brussels (50% consultancy) exist for the French-speaking half (Q23).
- Security learning curve: CyFun Small = 7 controls, Basic = 34 (Q39); DE/AT generalists do it (Q36).
- Verdict: **SERIOUS** for Flanders-first; **MANAGEABLE** for a Brussels/Wallonia-first start (smaller, less industrial market). "Flemish SMEs buy in Dutch only": Evidence not found beyond the thesis; INFERENCE that a non-Dutch-speaking seller will not close Flemish CEOs.

### H7. Price anchors
- Free: Kube ESG (+AI, 2026), EcoVadis Vitals, Workable Method (members), CCB CyFun self-assessment + policy templates, CyFun Small, Agoria/Sirris Cyber Risk Scan, VSME Reporter/Eevery free tiers (VERIFIED, Q14/Q22/Q26/Q33/Q21). Paid tools: VSME EUR 199/yr-99/mo (deep dive); 1up USD 250-850/month with Dutch+French output, Starter 12 questionnaires/yr (VERIFIED, Q41); SecuroNova EUR 399/mo (Q37). Consultancies (Mantis, Kroll SR, vetosec, InstantSecure, wermescher): quote-only; EcoVadis first pass 20-40 h; consultant "saves 30-50 h" (Q16/Q38).
- Verdict: **SERIOUS**. ESG side is anchored at zero by the Belgian banking system; security side at USD 250/mo software or a per-questionnaire consultancy quote. A EUR 290/month retainer has to beat "free VSME + 1up".
- Mitigation: per-questionnaire pricing (EUR 500-1,500 for an 8-page NIS2 questionnaire = 1-2 days DE benchmark) rather than subscription.

### H8. Commoditised cited-RAG answering
- 1up in Dutch/French at USD 250/mo; Vanta Core (USD 10-12k) and Drata Foundation include questionnaire automation; Sunhat Proof AI; Kube ESG ships "Smart-formule" AI; open-source local-first cited-RAG questionnaire tools with BYO model exist on GitHub (VERIFIED, Q17/Q28/Q41).
- Verdict: **FATAL for "AI agent" as the differentiator; IRRELEVANT for the service** — agrees with the deep dive (commodity). The founder's Python/agent learning is a cost centre, not a moat.

That is a side consultancy in an already-populated DE/AT/NL category, not a venture. The venture-scale variant (buyer-accepted passport) is structurally taken: EcoVadis + Kube ESG on ESG, CCB label + ISO 27001 on security. Nothing venture-scale survives for this founder.
