# Deep research — OPP-D: Brussels co-ownership energy-renovation financing packs

Date: 2026-09-11. Method: WebSearch only (44 queries run, FR/NL/EN/DE; pages not opened; session search budget exhausted before the last 7 planned queries). Builds on U-067/U-068 (ci-batch-4.md), C-11/C-12 (stream-C.md) and belgium-founder-facts.md. Labels: VERIFIED (search-summary) / ESTIMATE / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / Evidence not found. Every VERIFIED fact carries its URL.

Thesis under test: per-building "AG financing packs" (per-lot capex allocation from quotités, scenarios reserve-fund vs collective loan vs individual loans, prime/ECORENO stacking, cash-call schedules) sold to professional syndics, PEB experts and renovation facilitators in Brussels ahead of the 2030/2033 PEB deadlines; later a SaaS simulator and possibly a financing marketplace.

HEADLINE (details below): three premises of the thesis are weakened by 2026 facts: (a) Renolution primes are gone and will not return (replaced from 2027 by an unspecified 0% loan scheme), so "prime stacking" is empty in Brussels until 2027 rules exist; (b) ECORENO is explicitly NOT open to ACPs, so "ECORENO stacking" can only be per-owner for private shares; (c) the Region and the syndic federation already give free financing guidance to syndics (Facilitateur Copropriété attends AGs; Federia Syndic Reno Support trained 430+ syndics with Look&Fin as financing partner). What survives is a narrower, real workflow gap — per-lot, scenario-based, AG-ready financing tables that nobody produces cheaply — with unproven willingness to pay.

---

## 1. Exact problem and exact customer

Problem (VERIFIED components, INFERENCE on the whole):
- Brussels co-ownerships face a regulatory renovation cascade: single PEB expert per ACP, certificate per lot, synthesis report with renovation recommendations, unit-level thresholds (F/G by 2033, 150 kWh/m2.yr by 2045), fines for which the ACP is automatically co-liable if common-parts works were not undertaken (section 2.3). Works on common parts need an AG vote (2/3 majority for works since the 2019 reform — INFERENCE; one NL summary still cites the old 3/4 rule: https://www.dewaele.com/nl/advies/renoveren-in-mede-eigendom) and a financing decision (reserve fund, cash calls, ACP loan, individual loans).
- The financing decision is the recurring blocker: Bruxelles Environnement lists "cost of works and difficulty of finding financing" as the first brake, plus ageing owners who consider themselves too old for big works (VERIFIED, https://environnement.brussels/sites/default/files/user_files/210927_sem13_reno_copro_programme_fr_0.pdf ; https://www.moustique.be/notre-epoque/les-infos/2023/12/09/quels-sont-les-freins-a-la-renovation-energetique-des-batiments-PWXQ5LCARNCBDPB5AO7Y27SCXM/). Federia: "for some co-owners, even with appropriate credit, renovation costs remain prohibitive" (VERIFIED, https://syndicrenosupport.immo/fr/a-propos/renovation-energetique).
- Syndics have no cheap way to show each co-owner "what this costs you, under each financing route, per year" before the vote. Belgian syndic software (Syndic24, TheSyndic, Konvivio, FMT, SyndicPro) shows no renovation-financing or multi-year-plan module (VERIFIED absence in summaries, https://www.konvivio.be/fr/ ; https://syndic24.be/ ; https://www.thesyndic.com/ ; https://mysyndicpro.be/).

Customer:
- Primary buyer: professional syndic offices in Brussels. Brussels has 42,388 apartment buildings (2025) of which "slightly more than half" have a professional syndic (VERIFIED, BRUZZ 2026-06-24, https://www.bruzz.be/actua/samenleving/waarom-zoveel-mede-eigenaars-botsen-met-hun-syndicus-2026-06-24). IPI 2025: 1,835 real-estate agents in Brussels; nationally 672 syndic-only agents (+10% y/y) and 1,782 dual-licence agents (VERIFIED, https://www.ipi.be/rapports-annuels/2025/IPI-RAPPORT-ANNUEL-2025.pdf). Brussels has 74 members registered only in the syndic column (VERIFIED, BIV/BRUZZ). Typical office: e.g. La Gestion d'Immeubles SA manages 220+ buildings, 4,200 apartments, average 20 apartments/building (VERIFIED, https://gestionimmeubles.be/). ESTIMATE: 150-300 syndic offices active in Brussels (74 syndic-only members plus dual-licence agencies; office count not published).
- Who decides: the AG (co-owners) votes works and financing; the syndic prepares and executes; the conseil de copropriété supervises (VERIFIED general framework, https://syndic24.be/guide/regles-vote-majorites-ag/ ; https://la-copropriete.be/les-intervenants/lassemblee-generale/les-majorites/). The syndic decides whether to commission a pack; the AG must approve any cost charged to the ACP unless it fits an existing budget line or the syndic's contract (INFERENCE from "only services in the syndic contract can be billed": https://syncura.be/fr/votre-syndic-de-copropriete/syndic-de-copropriete/cout-syndic-copropriete).
- Who pays: three routes — (i) the ACP (as "frais d'étude" voted with the works or from the working capital; typical when a PEB expert/engineer is already paid per study); (ii) the syndic, absorbing it inside its works-follow-up fee (1.5-3% of works for admin/financial management; a 3% HTVA example is cited for Belgium: https://www.housing-service.be/syndic-de-copropriete-belgique/); (iii) the PEB expert/engineering office bundling it into its synthesis report or masterplan. INFERENCE: route (ii) is most frictionless (no AG vote), which caps price at a small fraction of the syndic's works fee.
- Secondary buyers: PEB experts (single expert per ACP is now mandatory), engineering offices producing renovation masterplans, and Federia/Bopro-type support services.

Dutch flag: Brussels syndics are bilingual by law of the market (Syndicoach markets "tweetalig VME-beheer", https://syndicoach.be/syndicus-brussel/); CIB represents 900+ professional syndics in Flanders and Brussels (VERIFIED, https://www.cib.be/sector-inzichten-overzicht/ae5be991-3ec8-4cfa-aeee-34f93aaeeacb). A Brussels-only pack can be FR-first, but any Flemish syndic client or Flanders expansion needs NL documents and NL AG narrative.

## 2. Evidence of demand

2.1 AG deadlock frequency
- Belgium-specific frequency: Evidence not found (both FR and NL queries returned procedural guides, not statistics). Best proxies:
  - France: "nearly one in three works projects rejected at AG; works resolutions are those most refused; financing is the main obstacle; it is not opponents but co-owners in financial difficulty who block; average voted works budget +9.5% in two years" (VERIFIED, https://www.koliving.fr/copropriete-travaux-un-sur-trois-rejete-renovation-2026/ ; https://www.esteval.fr/article.46202.expertises-renovation-energetique-en-copropriete-ce-n-est-pas-le-chantier-qui-bloque-c-est ; https://www.baticopro.com/guides/renovation-energetique-en-copropriete-comment-financer-un-projet-sans-bloquer-les-decisions.html).
  - Brussels qualitative: RTBF investigation on the F/G ban: renovation rate too slow, worsened by the subsidy pause and lack of communication since Aug 2024; risk of a renovation "bottleneck" in a labour-short region (VERIFIED, https://www.rtbf.be/article/interdiction-des-peb-f-et-g-a-bruxelles-notre-enquete-pointe-un-risque-d-embouteillages-des-renovations-11670616).
  - Federia analysis 2025 based on exchanges with 80+ syndics and 15 case studies; 750+ co-ownerships identified as having started renovation initiatives; 120+ projects supported (VERIFIED, https://syndicrenosupport.immo/fr/a-propos/renovation-energetique). INFERENCE: 750 of ~42,000 Brussels+Wallonia... (Federia covers Brussels and Wallonia) buildings is <2% engagement after two years of a federation-run programme — demand for renovation projects exists but is thin per year.
  - Flanders/NL: guidance sites stress "investigate financing and subsidies before the vote so each co-owner knows their contribution" (VERIFIED, https://vmewijzer.be/renovatie-en-energie/renovatie-van-een-appartementsgebouw-waar-let-uw-vme-best-op) — exactly the pack's job, framed as a syndic duty.

2.2 Number of co-ownerships
- Brussels: 42,388 apartment buildings in 2025 (30,000 in 2010; 37,419 in 2020). Communes: Brussels-City 6,334; Ixelles 4,228; Schaerbeek 4,091; Uccle 3,408; Anderlecht 3,018 (VERIFIED, https://www.bruzz.be/actua/samenleving/waarom-zoveel-mede-eigenaars-botsen-met-hun-syndicus-2026-06-24). Co-ownerships hold >50% of Brussels dwellings; 97% of co-ownership stock would remain to renovate by 2050 under the strategy's targets (VERIFIED, Bruxelles Environnement seminar doc above). Belgium: >90,000 co-ownerships (VERIFIED, IPI, stream C-11).
- Flanders: Embuild counts "13,000 energy-guzzling and dangerous flat buildings" (VERIFIED headline, https://www.embuildvlaanderen.be/press-room/13.000-energieverslindende-en-gevaarlijke-flatgebouwen/). Total Flemish apartment buildings: Evidence not found; ESTIMATE ~35-40k (Belgium >90k minus Brussels 42k minus Wallonia).

2.3 Brussels PEB-copropriété regulation status (Sept 2026)
- Timeline conflicts across sources; record both: (a) environnement.brussels/Renolution: every dwelling must hold a valid PEB certificate by 31 Dec 2030 (PIM: "1 Jan 2031"); F/G units (>275 kWh/m2.yr) must be renovated by 1 Jan 2033; D/E within 20 years of entry into force; 150 kWh/m2.yr by 31 Dec 2045 (VERIFIED, https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation-et-inspection/obligations-et-autorisations/la-peb-en-copropriete ; https://renolution.brussels/fr/news/la-reglementation-bruxelloise-acte-la-fin-des-batiments-energivores ; https://www.pim.be/peb-a-bruxelles-la-reforme-entre-en-vigueur/). (b) Ministerial order of 19 Feb 2026 (MB 9 Mar 2026) sets 1 Jul 2028 as entry into force of essential CoBrACE PEB provisions; from then every real-right holder has 5 years, i.e. by 1 Jul 2033, to hold a valid certificate (VERIFIED, https://be.andersen.com/fr/news/peb-bruxelles-chaque-batiment-devra-disposer-dun-certificat-de-performance-energetique-dici-le-1er-juillet-2033 ; https://www.certinergie.be/fr/2024/02/27/peb-renovation-bruxelles/). INFERENCE: the certificate deadline has effectively slid from end-2030 to mid-2033 for the new regime; the 2033 renovation threshold is the binding date. Verify in the MB text before quoting to customers.
- ACP duties: designate a single PEB expert who coordinates common-parts data, certifies each unit lacking a certificate and produces a synthesis report with renovation recommendations transmitted to Bruxelles Environnement; execution modalities (content/form of the report, coordination mission) still to be set by the Government (VERIFIED, https://environnement.brussels/pro/reglementation-et-inspection/obligations-et-autorisations/copropriete-la-gestion-de-la-peb ; https://www.syndicrenosupport.immo/fr/ressources/faqs/etude-technique-et-appel-d-offres/a-bruxelles-que-faire-pour-se-preparer-au-rapport-de-synthese-obligatoire). Since 19 Mar 2026 the synthesis report must be handed to the buyer on any apartment sale (VERIFIED, https://www.pim.be/bruxelles-le-certificat-peb-nest-plus-une-formalite/). A separate "certificat PEB collectif" for the whole building: Evidence not found — the regime is unit certificates + one building-level synthesis report by one expert (single calculation method software in 2026: https://document.environnement.brussels/opac_css/elecfile/Reforme_PEB_Indicateurs_Exigences_PEB).
- Sanctions: per non-compliant dwelling, fine = (gap to PEB target) x floor area x EUR 2.5; the ACP is automatically co-liable and pays the sum of unit fines if it has not undertaken the necessary common-parts works; a co-owner who proves good faith and efforts to get AG approval is exempt for their share (VERIFIED, https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation-et-inspection/obligations-et-autorisations/reglementation-peb-quelles-consequences-en-cas-de-non-respect ; https://www.federia.immo/fr/thematiques/2024-02-27-revision-du-cobrace-du-changement-en-matiere-de-peb-pour-les-coproprietes-a-bruxelles). INFERENCE: the "good-faith" exemption creates demand for documented AG proposals — a pack is evidence of diligence.
- Federia's July 2025 warning: individual certificates will tell an owner with a "D" that he is compliant for 2033, but the co-owner is not "safe" if common parts drag the building — a structural misalignment the pack could make visible (VERIFIED, https://www.federia.immo/fr/thematiques/2025-07-08-nouveaux-certificats-peb-a-bruxelles-des-avancees-importantes-mais-aussi-un-gros-point-d-attention-pour-les-appartements).
- PLAGE (large-consumer action plans) for co-ownerships: Evidence not found (query not run — budget).
- A statutory ACP "plan de rénovation" obligation beyond the expert's synthesis report: Evidence not found.

2.4 Subsidies and loans — uptake
- Renolution primes: envelope 2024 EUR 69M exhausted by July 2024; suspended 16 Aug 2024; no new applications for invoices dated 2025/2026; Dilliès government confirmed Feb 2026 no return; budget vote end March 2026; EUR 56M released to pay 2,700 pending files; replacement announced: zero-rate loans, EUR 66M/yr from 2027, modalities (ceilings, income conditions, per-work amounts) not fixed as of 2 Jul 2026; 6% VAT remains (VERIFIED, https://myrenov.be/primes/bruxelles/fin-primes-renolution/ ; https://pebify.be/primes-renolution-supprimees-financer-renovation-bruxelles/ ; https://batibouw.com/fr/articles/1228/fin-des-primes-renolution-prets-a-taux-zero-le-nouveau-cap-logement-a-bruxelles ; https://www.bruxellestoday.be/actualite/gouvernement-bruxellois-primes-renovation.html). Historical common-parts prime rules (single application via the ACP, up to 90% of invoiced cost, cap EUR 200,000 for common parts) are now moot (VERIFIED historical, https://homegrade.brussels/fiches-infos/primes-renolution-pour-coproprietes/).
- ECORENO: not accessible to landlords nor to ACPs (VERIFIED, https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/services-et-demandes/primes-et-aides-financieres/le-credit-ecoreno ; https://www.easy-primes.be/blog/fr-1/prets-et-solutions-de-financement-pour-vos-travaux-de-renovation-energetique-en-copropriete-34). Fonds du Logement suspended all new credits from 1 Jul 2025; ECORENO reopened 1 Jan 2026 at 2.5%/3.5% (EUR 1,500-25,000 unsecured up to 10 yrs, or mortgage up to 120% of post-works value up to 30 yrs); full activity resumed 1 Jul 2026 under a EUR 100M regional guarantee; staff on economic unemployment from 1 Mar 2026; 678 ECORENO credits granted in 2025 (VERIFIED, https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/04/23/suspendus-depuis-lete-2025-les-credits-hypothecaires-du-fonds-du-logement-doivent-reprendre-en-juillet-Z7VJSNXN7NHGDAXO6F5OQSQBIQ/ ; https://bx1.be/categories/news/le-gouvernement-bruxellois-accorde-une-garantie-de-100-millions-deuros-pour-relancer-le-fonds-du-logement/ ; https://fonds.brussels/fr/a-propos/nouvelles-fonds-du-logement/notre-rapport-dactivites-2025-est-en-ligne ; https://myrenov.be/primes/bruxelles/credit-ecoreno/). ECORENO loans to ACPs: zero by design.
- ACP loans available in Belgium: KBC/KBC Brussels VME renovation credit (since 2015, with credit insurance) (VERIFIED, https://www.kbcbrussels.be/ondernemen/nl/product/kredieten/renovatiekrediet-voor-vme.html ; https://www.dvo.be/artikel/53635-kbc-lanceert-renovatiekrediet-voor-vmes); Belfius ACP credit (VERIFIED existence, https://www.belfius.be/common/FR/multimedia/MMDownloadableFile/Business/Financing/MMI%20krediet%20VME.pdf — decision by absolute majority authorises the syndic to sign); Look&Fin ACP loan: online application by the syndic, offer within 15 days, fixed rate valid 9 months, funds released after non-contestation attestation of the AG decision and a 30% invoice; eligibility: Belgian seat, professional or volunteer syndic, mainly residential, <5% of co-owners >180 days in arrears; example 60 months at 6.25% (VERIFIED, https://www.lookandfin.com/fr/credit-ACP ; https://www.lookandfin.com/fr_company_info/actualite/renovation-energetique-look-fin-lance-le-pret-pour-les-coproprietes). Look&Fin total 2025 origination EUR 67M over 105 operations, all segments (VERIFIED, https://www.lookandfin.com/fr/actualite/pret-immobilier-2025). ACP-loan volumes per lender: Evidence not found. Wallonia's Rénoprêt (0% for ACPs, FLW) exists but not in Brussels (VERIFIED, https://www.flw.be/emprunter/travaux/association-de-coproprietaires/).
- Flanders: Mijn VerbouwLening for VMEs: up to EUR 60,000 + EUR 25,000 per apartment, 25 years, 0%/0.5%/1.5% by income category, mandatory Atradius credit insurance; co-owners who do not want to join must notify the syndic within 14 days of the AG (VERIFIED, https://www.mechelen.be/wonen-en-verbouwen/bouwen-en-verbouwen/leningen-premies-en-subsidies-voor-bouwen-en-verbouwen/mijn-verbouwlening-voor-vmes ; https://www.igean.be/mijn-verbouwlening-voor-vmes ; https://callmepower.be/nl/verhuizen/energie/mijnverbouwlening). Application window: one source says until 30/06/2026, another "until end 2026" (conflict; verify).
- Netherlands (analogue): Nationaal Warmtefonds granted 22,248 Energiebespaarleningen Jan-Aug 2026 but only 158 to VvE's (144 same period 2025); it assesses reserve fund, MJOP status, payment behaviour and contribution-to-cost ratio (VERIFIED, https://www.harmsbakx.nl/artikelen/hypotheek/energiebespaarlening-2026-22248-leningen ; https://www.warmtefonds.nl/vve/voorwaarden-vve-energiebespaarlening). INFERENCE: even with a national 0% fund, collective loans are rare (~200/yr for ~125k Dutch VvE's); collective-loan uptake is structurally slow.

## 3. Willingness to pay

- PEB certificates Brussels: EUR 170-240 TVAC per apartment; whole-building offers from EUR 305/unit for 2 units, per-unit price + EUR 130 for 3+ units, 10-20% degressive; audit-style recommendations "included at no extra cost" (VERIFIED, https://blog.trustup.be/fr/prix-certificat-peb/ ; https://www.alenergy.be/certificat-peb-immeuble-bruxelles.html ; https://www.alenergy.be/certificat-peb-copropriete-bruxelles.html). INFERENCE: syndics are used to paying a few hundred euros per unit for regulatory paper; a EUR 500-1,500 building-level financing pack is inside that habit.
- Brussels "audit énergétique copropriété"/engineering-office study prices: Evidence not found (the public quickscan is free).
- Flanders renovatiemasterplan (best Belgian analogue of a full technical + financial plan): EUR 20,000-40,000 incl. VAT per building; ~EUR 20-25k for a 30-unit building; VEKA pays up to EUR 12,000 (max 60%) for buildings >=15 units through six selected bureaus; applications until end 2025, to be extended via a new framework agreement (VERIFIED, https://www.eigenaarsbond.be/tip/het-renovatiemasterplan/ ; https://03beheer.be/blog/renovatiemasterplan-hulp-voor-ambitieuze-gebouwen/ ; https://trema.be/renovatiemasterplan/ ; https://publicaties.vlaanderen.be/view-file/83527). INFERENCE: VMEs pay EUR 8-16k net for a masterplan that includes financing scenarios — proof that co-ownerships pay for planning when subsidised and when an engineering firm is the vendor.
- France PPPT (technical plan + collective DPE): EUR 2,000-20,000; EUR 150-400 per apartment; typical bands EUR 2,800-3,000 (<10 lots), 3,000-3,500 (10-25), 3,500-4,300 (25-50), 4,300-6,500 HT (50-100) (VERIFIED, https://opera-energie.com/plan-pluriannuel-de-travaux-prix/ ; https://thermiconseil.fr/actualites/plan-pluriannuel-de-travaux-prix/ ; https://copropriete.hellio.com/blog/renovation-energetique/prix-plan-pluriannuel-travaux). INFERENCE: the financing/scenario layer is perhaps 15-30% of a PPPT's value -> EUR 500-1,500 per building is the plausible Brussels ceiling for a financing-only pack; larger buildings tolerate more.
- Syndic ability to bill it: extras must be in the contract or AG-approved (VERIFIED above). The syndic's own works fee (1.5-3% of, say, EUR 300k works = EUR 4,500-9,000) is the natural budget from which EUR 500-1,500 for a pack can be paid without an AG vote — HYPOTHESIS to test in interviews.
- Federia's FAQ answers financing questions for free and Look&Fin does loan structuring for free (as origination); the Region's facilitators attend AGs for free (VERIFIED existence). This suppresses WTP for advice; WTP remains only for the labour-saving deliverable (tables, per-lot schedules, scenario comparison) — INFERENCE.

## 4. Bottom-up TAM / SAM / SOM

Assumptions are labelled; all money figures ESTIMATE/HYPOTHESIS.
- Brussels buildings: 42,388 (VERIFIED). With professional syndic: ~55% -> ~23,000 (ESTIMATE from "slightly more than half").
- Share needing collective works before 2033: Evidence not found for building-level F/G share. HYPOTHESIS 40-60% -> 9,000-14,000 pro-syndic buildings with a decision to finance before 2033; i.e. ~1,300-2,000 per year over 7 years if evenly spread (it will not be: bunching in 2029-2032 is likely).
- Price per pack: EUR 600 (<15 lots), 1,000 (15-40), 1,500 (>40) -> blended ~EUR 1,000 (ESTIMATE anchored on sections 3).
- TAM Brussels (one pack per pro-syndic building over 7 years): 23,000 x EUR 1,000 = EUR 23M cumulative, ~EUR 3M/yr. Realistic TAM (buildings that actually plan works): 9,000-14,000 x EUR 1,000 = EUR 9-14M cumulative, EUR 1.3-2M/yr.
- SAM (buildings >=10 lots managed by offices that outsource; assume 60% of the above): EUR 0.8-1.2M/yr.
- SOM for a part-time founder (10-15 syndic offices, 15-30 packs each per year at maturity): 150-450 packs/yr x EUR 1,000 = EUR 150-450k/yr (HYPOTHESIS; requires automation — see section 12). Year-1 SOM realistically 20-40 packs = EUR 20-40k.
- Add-on: re-runs each AG season (updated quotes, rates, arrears) at 30-40% of pack price -> recurring layer, HYPOTHESIS.
- Flanders: 13,000 "problematic" flat buildings (VERIFIED headline) plus a public financing tool and subsidised masterplans in the pipeline; SAM there depends on whether VEKA ships its tool. ESTIMATE EUR 1-2M/yr addressable if no public tool; near zero for a paid standalone if it ships. Wallonia: Rénoprêt 0% + Federia support; smaller stock; not sized.

## 6. Why now; why not incumbents; why syndics would pay

Why now (VERIFIED drivers): single-expert + synthesis-report regime live in 2026; synthesis report required at every apartment sale since 19 Mar 2026; F/G threshold 1 Jan 2033; ACP co-liability for fines; prime regime collapsed (Aug 2024) and its replacement (0% loans, EUR 66M/yr) starts 2027 with unknown rules — making financing scenarios more, not less, complex in 2026-2028; the Fonds du Logement itself was frozen for a year. Counter-signal: the 19 Feb 2026 order pushed core PEB provisions to 1 Jul 2028, i.e. the pressure is real but the calendar has slipped once already and may slip again (INFERENCE).

Why incumbents/public bodies have not: public bodies give advice, not per-building financial engineering, and are budget-constrained (primes gone, Fonds on economic unemployment); Federia is a federation, not a software vendor; lenders only model their own product; syndic software vendors serve accounting with EUR 2-4/lot pricing and no incentive to build a low-frequency module; engineering offices price technical work at EUR 20-40k and have no interest in a EUR 1k deliverable (INFERENCE).

Why syndics would pay: (i) it de-risks the AG (one-in-three rejection rate in FR; financing the top brake in BXL); (ii) it documents good faith for the fine exemption; (iii) the syndic bills 1.5-3% of works and needs the vote to earn it; (iv) capacity: syndic is a shortage profession (VERIFIED, stream C-11). Why they might not: facilitators attend AGs for free; Federia trains them to do it themselves; low frequency per building; AG once a year.

## 8. Belgium analysis

- Brussels advantage: densest co-ownership stock in Belgium (42k buildings, >50% of dwellings), hardest deadlines (2033 F/G ban), ACP co-liability for fines, and the most disrupted subsidy landscape (primes gone, Fonds frozen 2025-26, new 0% loans 2027 with unknown rules) — highest complexity, thus highest value of scenario modelling (INFERENCE). Founder is on site and FR-fluent.
- FR vs NL: Brussels pro syndics are bilingual; the syndic-only column has 74 members in Brussels; CIB (NL) represents 900+ syndics in Flanders+Brussels; sector content is split between Federia (FR, https://www.federia.immo/) and CIB/BIV (NL). Dutch matters for: (i) any Flemish-rooted Brussels syndic; (ii) Flanders where the public tool risk is highest; (iii) NL-language AG narratives in mixed buildings.
- Regional differences: Flanders has 0% VME loans (with Atradius insurance), subsidised masterplans, EPC of common parts mandatory since 2024 with EUR 500-5,000 fines, a renovation duty for E/F units bought since 2023 (D within 5-6 years) and a government-announced financing tool (VERIFIED, https://certiloket.be/kennisbank/epc-gemeenschappelijke-delen/ ; https://certipoint.be/de-renovatieverplichting-in-2026-van-epc-label-f-naar-d-in-5-jaar/). Wallonia has the FLW Rénoprêt 0% for ACPs (feasibility study + 4-month AG delay) (VERIFIED, https://www.immopass.eu/blog/2024-03-renopret-la-solution-a-taux-zero-pour-les-associations-de-coproprietaires). Brussels has the least public collective financing today — the messiest and therefore the best market for a private scenario pack, but also the poorest customers' co-owners.
- Subsidies that could pay for the pack: Brussels consultancy subsidy suspended since 12 Aug 2026 (belgium-founder-facts); KMO-portefeuille consulting now limited to digital/cyber (belgium-founder-facts) — no subsidy lever for the pack itself.

## 9. EU expansion

- France: PPPT mandatory since 1 Jan 2025 for all co-ownerships >15 years; collective loan with automatic adhesion (law 9 Apr 2024) (VERIFIED, https://www.my-syndic.com/blog/plan-pluriannuel-de-travaux-ppt-ce-que-votre-copropriete-doit-savoir-en-2026 ; https://www.afcopro.com/pret-collectif-a-adhesion-automatique-une-avancee-majeure-pour-financer-les-travaux-en-copropriete-mais-sous-certaines-conditions/). Market is large but already served by PPPT software and Matera-type syndics; MaPrimeRénov' Copropriété rules (35% gain, RGE, AMO, EUR 25k/dwelling ceiling) are a different engine. What changes: rules engine, lender products, and heavy competition; a Belgian entrant has no edge.
- Netherlands: VvE's with mandatory MJOP and reserve fund; Warmtefonds VvE loan; only ~160 VvE loans in 8 months of 2026 (VERIFIED) — slow uptake; language NL; Dutch VvE-software (Twinq etc., not researched) likely covers MJOP. What changes: MJOP is the anchor document, not PEB; subsidy SVVE.
- Germany: WEG reform 2020 — energetic renovations and Sonderumlagen by simple majority; the Verwalter must supply cost estimates and a financing plan; KfW 261/458 and BEG EM (VERIFIED, https://www.weg-wissen.de/sonderumlage-weg-beschluss-hoehe-zahlungspflicht-2026/ ; https://magazin.hausverwalterscout.de/sanierung-weg/). What changes: simple-majority regime lowers the deadlock problem; German Hausverwaltung software is deep; language.
- Conclusion: the product logic (per-lot allocation + scenarios + subsidy stacking) is portable; the rules engine, lender set and channel are not. Expansion is a re-founding per country, not a roll-out.

## 10. AI architecture and build effort

Inputs: PEB certificates/synthesis report (PDF), contractor quotes (PDF), acte de base/quotités table (PDF/Excel), syndic accounts (reserve fund, working capital, arrears), lender term sheets (rate, tenor, insurance cost), public rules (2027 0% loan rules when published, 6% VAT, Flemish loan caps).
Architecture:
1. Extraction layer (LLM with structured output): quotités per lot, unit surfaces/labels, quote line items, cost splits common/private — with human review UI (a spreadsheet is enough for MVP).
2. Deterministic financial engine (Python/pandas or Excel): capex phasing; allocation by quotités or by special keys; scenarios: reserve fund draw-down + cash calls; ACP loan (annuity, insurance, per-lot share, opt-out handling per the Flemish 14-day rule); individual loans (ECORENO 2.5/3.5%, bank); mixed; per-lot annual cash impact; ACP cash-flow and covenant checks (<5% arrears for Look&Fin); fine-exposure estimate (gap x m2 x EUR 2.5) as the "do-nothing" scenario.
3. Narrative layer (LLM): FR/NL AG-ready summary, resolution wording, FAQ for co-owners — generated from engine outputs only (no numbers invented by the LLM).
4. Output: PDF pack + Excel annex per lot.
Build effort (ESTIMATE): manual Excel version 20-40 h; Python engine + templates 80-150 h; extraction with review 40-80 h; web SaaS with accounts 300-600 h (out of founder reach part-time in year 1 without contractors). Founder fit is strong on layers 2-3; weak on 4.

## 11. AI tests

- AI leverage: MEDIUM. The engine is classic FP&A; AI adds speed on extraction and narrative (maybe 50-70% of pack hours). Classification: AI-accelerated service, not AI-native.
- AI commoditisation: HIGH RISK. A syndic with a general LLM can already draft the narrative; extraction is a commodity; the deterministic model is a few hundred lines. Any syndic-software vendor, Federia (RENO TOOLBOX in-agency version) or VEKA can replicate. Defensibility must come from data access, trust, and lender integration, not the AI.
- AGI test (assume near-human agents cheaply available): the pack becomes free; residual value = the syndic relationship, liability-bearing signature, and a regulated marketplace position. Classification: value migrates to distribution/regulation; the standalone product fails.
- ASI test: irrelevant differences; regulated financing and human AG dynamics remain the bottleneck; the business is not an AI bet.

## 12. Business model and unit economics

- Pricing (ESTIMATE): EUR 600 / 1,000 / 1,500 per pack by size (<15 / 15-40 / >40 lots); AG-season refresh EUR 300-500; optional per-office subscription EUR 150-300/month for unlimited refreshes once automated (HYPOTHESIS).
- Hours per pack: manual 8-12 h (first 5), semi-automated 3-5 h, automated 1.5-2.5 h incl. review (ESTIMATE). Direct cost near zero (LLM API a few euros). Gross margin >90%; the constraint is founder hours.
- Seasonality: AGs in spring (March-May) (VERIFIED, https://syndic24.be/guide/assemblee-generale-organisation/); convocations 15 days before; packs must be ready Jan-Apr; selling happens Sep-Dec. Extraordinary AGs (EUR 200-600) can be called for works — a pack can justify one.
- Capacity: 10-15 h/week -> ~1 manual pack/week -> ~35-45 packs/yr -> EUR 35-45k/yr gross; automation lifts to 3-4/week in season.
- EUR 1k/month: 1-2 packs/month from 2-3 syndic offices — reachable in year 1 if 3 offices adopt.

Classification: not a EUR 100k/month business on current evidence.
- Four capital scenarios: (A) EUR 0-2k: sole trader, Excel/Python, manual packs — recommended; (B) EUR 10-25k: legal opinion (FSMA/IPI), NL translation, light web front-end by a contractor, 40 packs/yr target; (C) EUR 50-100k: SaaS + part-time salesperson, Flanders launch, lender co-branding; (D) EUR 250k+: FSMA-registered marketplace, integrations with FMT/Exact, France — only if (A)-(B) show >=10 paying offices and re-order rates >50%.

## 16. Main risks and reasons NOT to pursue; evidence that would flip the recommendation

Reasons not to pursue:
1. Public substitution: Facilitateur Copropriété attends AGs for free; Federia trains syndics in-agency; Flanders is building a financing tool; Brussels may bundle a simulator with the 2027 0% loans (Evidence not found either way).
2. Subsidy vacuum: with primes gone and 2027 rules unknown, packs made in 2026 are provisional; the "stacking" value is thin until 2027.
3. Low frequency and long cycle: one pack per building per decade; one AG season per year; slippage risk (core provisions already pushed to Jul 2028).
4. Thin realistic TAM (EUR 1.3-2M/yr Brussels) and no path to EUR 100k/month without a regulated marketplace.
5. Liability/trust: errors in per-lot tables at an AG; professional-liability cost unknown.
6. Channel is occupied (Federia + Bopro + Look&Fin) and public bodies will not endorse paid tools.
7. Collective-loan uptake is structurally low (NL: ~160 VvE loans/yr; BE lender volumes unknown), reducing marketplace upside.

Evidence that would change the recommendation (to GO): >=3 Brussels syndic offices pre-committing to pay EUR 800+ per pack for spring 2027; Federia or Look&Fin agreeing to list/co-brand; publication of 2027 0% loan rules that are per-owner and income-tiered (raising per-lot modelling complexity) without a public simulator; data showing >30% of Brussels apartment buildings have F/G units needing common-parts works. (To NO-GO): Bruxelles Environnement or the Fonds announces a co-ownership financing simulator; a syndic software vendor ships a renovation-financing module; interviews show syndics rely on the free facilitator for AG financing slides.
