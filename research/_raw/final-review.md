## 2. Attack on the #1 pick (OPP-A)

**2.1 It won by survivorship, and the scoring made a services idea the only possible winner.** Entry realism (29) + founder/interest fit (17) = 46 of 100 points. Large-company potential carries 3 points, market size 3. Any monthly retainer that the founder can start with EUR 500 scores 9-10 on capital, time-to-revenue and Belgium launchability before a single customer exists. OPP-A did not beat venture-shaped ideas; the screen (universe section 2, rule (c) "founder can reach the buyer", rule (e) "fits a finance founder") removed them before scoring, and the weights finished the job. See section 4.

**2.2 The steelman removes the founder's differentiator, then asks him to compete on price with freelancers.** The universe pitch (U-031/U-021) was "controller + agent tooling + Odoo/Exact-native product". The red team killed the product (Odoo 19 auto-eliminations, Odoo 20 previews, Finstack EUR 39/entity, Silverfin group consolidation with IC elimination in the firm channel — Q6, Q9). What is left is a part-time ex-corporate controller selling group-reporting hours against an abundant supply of full-time freelance controllers at EUR 650-900/day (Q20, Apex League). The claimed "asymmetric advantage" (HQ formats, IC discipline) is the standard skill set of every Big-4 alumnus and ex-group-controller on the interim market; it is not asymmetric. The only real asymmetry — the tooling — is what the plan now says not to build before Odoo 20 confirms the gap. INFERENCE.

**2.3 The revenue model rests on a cadence nobody has evidenced.** Six queries across the programme (five by the red team, one here, Q22) failed to find a single Belgian SME group paying monthly for outsourced management consolidation; every outsourced-consolidation price found is annual/statutory (PwC CaaS, Vandelanotte, VGD, financialservices.lu in Luxembourg, Q4/Q9). The plan's own kill-signal ("majority say quarterly is enough") is, on current evidence, the base case. Quarterly cadence halves the revenue (EUR 900-1,500/quarter tier) and turns 4-6 clients into EUR 15-35k/yr, i.e. a freelance top-up, not EUR 70-200k.

**2.4 The unit economics are a year-2 tooling assumption presented as the business.** EUR 2,000/month at 5 h/client = EUR 400/h effective only after the ingestion/mapping/IC/rendering stack exists (170-240 h of build, 4-6 months at 10 h/week, per deep dive). Year 1 is 10-14 h/client/month = EUR 140-200/h, which is exactly the freelance controller rate. The client will benchmark the fixed pack against "two controller days" (EUR 1,300-1,800), so the founder's margin is capped by the interim day rate unless the pack is demonstrably more than two days of work — which, for a 3-8-entity group with clean IC coding, it is not. ESTIMATE/INFERENCE.

**2.5 The close-week collision is not "manageable"; it is structural for this founder.** A corporate FP&A controller's WD1-WD5 is the same week his clients want group numbers. The plan's answer (only clients with WD10+ or quarterly deadlines) narrows an already unevidenced segment further and excludes the foreign-subsidiary segment, whose HQ calendars are fixed by HQ, not by the client.

**2.6 Subsidy asymmetry cuts against the founder, not for him.** Walloon chèques-entreprises labelling requires >=3 years as a consultant and ~EUR 50k/yr or ~65 service days/yr over the previous three years, in a legal entity (Q21). Flemish kmo-portefeuille advice is cyber-only; Brussels consultancy subsidy is suspended since 12 Aug 2026 (deep dive). Registered ITAA firms and established freelancers can sell subsidised days; the founder cannot for 2-3 years. final-recommendation.md still lists "Wallonia digital-maturity vouchers may cover onboarding" as an advantage.

**2.7 One of the three "why now" pillars is a vendor-blog artefact.** "Interim rates face upward pressure (Dosign equal-pay ruling, 1 Jan 2026, VERIFIED)" (top-20.md, competitive-analysis.md, deep-OPP-A.md). The Dosign judgment is the Dutch Hoge Raad WAADI ruling of 27 Sep 2024, implemented through the NL ABU-cao on 1 Jan 2026 (Q1). It concerns Dutch uitzendkrachten; it does not govern Belgian freelance controllers, who are not temporary agency workers. The Belgian agency Apex League repeats it in its blog (Q20), which is where the deep dive took it. VERIFIED (search-summary) that the ruling is Dutch; the Belgian "rates rising" tailwind is therefore Evidence not found.

**2.8 The legal gate is a coin-flip with a bad tail.** The red team's reading of art. 3, 5° (internal profitability/risk analysis "not leading to an external attestation" is the reserved case) is harsher than the deep dive's and is the reading ITAA's own prosecution practice would favour. If the opinion goes that way, the residual is pure subcontracting under an ITAA firm's engagement letter at firm-set rates — i.e. the founder becomes a freelance controller for an accounting firm. That is a job, not a business.

**2.9 Is "lifestyle income" honest or defeatist?** Honest about OPP-A as specified: on the evidence, it is a part-time controlling practice with a EUR 200k/yr solo ceiling and no moat until a licence exists. Defeatist as a programme conclusion, for one specific reason: the final recommendation states that "the path to a large company, if it exists, runs through becoming an agent-first firm with an ITAA signatory" — and that exact idea (U-019, "BE/LU agent-native bookkeeping firm") was rejected at SCREEN with "requires ITAA-licensed practice or partner; capital-heavy" and never investigated, scored, deep-dived or red-teamed. The programme's own venture path was killed by a one-line screening note before the ownership rule (Q8) and the seven-year exemption (Q2, Q17) were discovered. That is the gap this review fills in section 3.

**Attack verdict.** OPP-A is a defensible cash engine only in its quarterly/trigger form and only if the art. 3 opinion is favourable; it is the least-dead idea among the 40 scored, not the best use of the founder's 10-15 h/week if the goal is a company. Its real value is as (i) a EUR 1-3k/month income and (ii) the client base and tooling that a licensed firm would later need.

## 6. Final verdict

**(c) Start OPP-A as the cash engine, in its quarterly/trigger form, and run one venture experiment in parallel: the ITAA-partnered agent-first firm (A1).**

OPP-A, as amended by this review: sell the quarterly "group reporting and IC discipline" tier first (it matches the only cadence with evidence), target PE-backed buy-and-build portfolio companies and no-EPM subsidiaries where the buyer's own calendar mandates monthly numbers (A8), do not build the ingestion stack before Odoo 20 is seen on 24-26 Sep 2026, and treat the art. 3 opinion, the employer authorisation and the ITAA seven-year-exemption eligibility question as three free-or-cheap gates to clear in the first two weeks. Expect EUR 1-3k/month within six months, not EUR 70-200k/yr.

The venture experiment (A1), 30-day test, EUR 0-100, 15-20 hours:
1. Week 1: submit the ITAA seven-year-exemption eligibility question with CV and task description (free; art. 14 form). Outcome A: the founder himself can sit the aptitude exam within 12-18 months and become the signatory. Outcome B: he needs a partner.
2. Weeks 1-4: ten conversations with certified accountants (ITAA, age 30-45, own practice of 2-10 staff, Brussels/Walloon Brabant/Namur, currently refusing clients) sourced via LinkedIn, one AccountancyVandaag or ITAA regional event, and the founder's own accountant. Pitch: a jointly owned firm (professional majority per the 2019 law) that takes their client stops with agent tooling and the founder's group-reporting practice; the founder is the minority tech/ops partner and stays employed until revenue justifies otherwise.
3. Week 4: one written term-sheet sketch (ownership, governance, capital of EUR 5-20k, the founder's OPP-A clients as the first controlling book).
Success: exemption eligible OR >=2 accountants willing to discuss a term sheet, AND Odoo 20 does not remove the reporting layer the firm would sell. Kill: exemption ineligible AND 0/10 accountants interested; then OPP-A stays a side income and U-004 becomes the 2027 build.

**Three strongest reasons.**
1. **The licence is the only moat the programme found in 102 ideas, and the law lets a non-member co-own it.** Every software layer was absorbed by Odoo, Microsoft, Visma/Silverfin, Chift; every solo service hit art. 3. A registered firm needs professionals to hold the majority of votes and board seats (Q8) — nothing more. The seven-year route (Q2, Q17) can make the founder himself the signatory. The team discovered both facts but only used them to justify a lifestyle income.
2. **The slot is open and VC-validated, with a visible clock.** No Belgian AI-native accounting firm surfaced in two searches (Q3, Q24); the Ghent cluster arms firms rather than competing with them; 56% of firms refuse clients. Neno (EUR 6.6M, Aug 2026) names Belgium for its 2027 wave (Q18): the model is investable, and the Belgian window is roughly 12-18 months.
3. **The experiment costs nothing that OPP-A needs.** It uses the same three gates OPP-A already requires (legal read, employer authorisation, exemption question), it converts OPP-A's clients and tooling into the firm's first controlling book, and it removes the single biggest weakness of OPP-A — that its labour revenue is AGI-destroyed and its "asymmetric advantage" is not asymmetric — by adding the one asset that regulation, not software, defends.

Reviewer's confidence: A1 is a HYPOTHESIS built on VERIFIED legal and market facts; it has not been interviewed, and its economics (firm valuations, partner split, Neno competition) are untested. OPP-A's amended form is the safer floor. The dishonest option is the one the team stopped short of: recording "agent-first firm with a signatory" as the only venture path while leaving it in the rejected table.
