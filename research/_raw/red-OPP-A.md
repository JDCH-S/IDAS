URL: https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/wet/2019/03/17/2019040805/justel ; https://etaamb.openjustice.be/nl/wet-van-17-maart-2019_n2019040805.html . Reading: analysis of profitability/risks *for internal use* is literally the reserved case. Variance commentary on group profitability = "analysis of rentabiliteit with accounting techniques". The counter-argument (art. 3 lists activities of the certified accountant's profession; not every activity in the list is exclusive — art. 117 penalises exercising the *profession*, ITAA's own FAQ says "financial advice/assisting companies financially" is free) needs a lawyer.
