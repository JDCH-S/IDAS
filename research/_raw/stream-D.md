## 2. AI-adoption gap evidence

1. **Skills is the #1 stated barrier, ahead of cost and regulation.** Among EU enterprises that considered AI but did not adopt, 70.9% cited lack of relevant skills/expertise; across EU/OECD/UK/G7 surveys 50–71% of non-adopters cite lack of expertise as the primary barrier. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.electe.net/en/post/ai-adoption-european-sme-barriers ; https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises_9c48eae6/426399c1-en.pdf
2. **SMEs adopt AI before digitising the finance base.** Eurostat/Qonto 2025: 46% of SMEs use AI tools like ChatGPT but only ~25% have adopted digital accounting solutions — AI sits on top of paper/PDF/Excel processes. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.electe.net/en/post/ai-adoption-european-sme-barriers
3. **AI lands on visible tasks, not back-office.** OECD: SMEs first apply AI to writing/summarising/sales, not to "less conspicuous but more profitable" data-quality, system-integration and workflow-standardisation work. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/empowering-smes-in-the-age-of-ai_bf5a9816-en.html
4. **Belgium: usage explodes, strategy lags.** A May 2026 Belgian study: KMO AI usage went from 8% (2024) to 81% (2026), but 51% "do not know how to deploy AI concretely and effectively". Agoria 2025: 24.7% of Belgian companies use AI (≈2x EU average); 34.5% of Belgian firms ≥10 employees used ≥1 AI technology in 2025. — VERIFIED (search-summary); note the 8%→81% figure is from one vendor-adjacent survey and the two surveys use different definitions — treat as ESTIMATE. https://belgiumcloud.com/2026/05/28/onderzoek-ai-gebruik-explodeert-bij-belgische-kmos-maar-strategische-inzet-blijft-achter/ ; https://blog.oeccbb.be/nl/article/ai-gebruik-in-belgische-bedrijven-blijft-sterk-groeien-1-op-de-3-ondernemingen-zet-ai-actief-in/29798 ; https://clutch.co/be/consulting/ai
5. **CFOs: deployed, not valued.** Gartner 2026: 84% of finance orgs have implemented or plan AI, only 7% report high impact; 84% of CFOs have not realised ROI; ~60% will raise finance-AI budgets ≥10% in 2026; 47% spend only 1–5% of finance-tech budget on AI; "acquiring and developing AI/digital talent" is CFOs' top near-term challenge; legacy tech, skills and employee adoption are the persistent barriers. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-28-gartner-says-cfos-must-stop-mistaking-finance-ai-deployment-for-value-creation ; https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-23-gartner-survey-reveals-acquiring-and-developing-ai-and-digital-talent-is-cfos-top-near-term-challenge ; https://www.cfodive.com/news/cfos-ai-adoption-slows-challenges-mount-gartner/805949/
6. **95% of GenAI pilots deliver no P&L impact (MIT, 2025)**; back-office deployments beat customer-facing ones; external, customised vendor partnerships succeed ~2x as often as internal builds. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.forbes.com/sites/jasonsnyder/2025/08/26/mit-finds-95-of-genai-pilots-fail-because-companies-avoid-friction/ ; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mit-report-95-generative-ai-105412686.html
7. **What SMEs pay instead: agencies.** Belgian/Dutch "AI agencies" (ClickForest, LTC Group, Aïves, Crux Digits, Build-More) sell project setup + monthly retainers; visible price points: Crux Digits audit €2,500, PoC €20,000, production integration from €50,000; generic ranges "€5k–€20k small integrations, €50k+ custom". — VERIFIED (search-summary) for the Crux Digits figures; the ranges are ESTIMATE. https://cruxdigits.nl/ai-automation/ ; https://www.clickforest.com/en/ai-for-growth ; https://www.ltcai.be/en ; https://clutch.co/be/consulting/ai
8. **EU AI Act cost for most SMEs is small; the fear is larger than the bill.** Estimates: non-high-risk deployers <€2k/yr; high-risk deployers €20–50k initial + €5–15k/yr; high-risk *providers* (incl. fintech) €200–600k initial + €80–150k/yr. Organisations report up to ~40% increase in compliance burden. Bulk of obligations apply Aug 2026. — ESTIMATE (vendor/consultancy figures in search summaries). https://ovidiusuciu.com/eu-ai-act-compliance-costs-smes-2026-and-beyond/ ; https://sqmagazine.co.uk/eu-ai-act-compliance-cost-statistics/ ; https://www.delbion.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-sme-compliance-guide/
9. **Peppol mandate (BE, 1 Jan 2026) turned master-data quality into a compliance problem.** Banqup/EY: wrong VAT numbers or incomplete master data cause invoice rejections and delayed payments; readiness uneven across industries; infrastructure "technically stable" but "operational maturity still evolving"; many SMEs' ERP/invoicing tools still lack Peppol support. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.banqup.com/resources/blog/belgium-s-2026-e-invoicing-mandate-what-have-we-learned-so-far- ; https://www.ey.com/en_be/insights/tax/how-belgiums-2026-e-invoicing-mandate-will-reshape-your-business
10. **France e-invoicing reform live 1 Sep 2026** is explicitly cited as the driver for accounting firms to modernise tools (Pennylane raised €175M partly to prepare for it). — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://siecledigital.fr/2026/01/20/pennylane-soffre-175-millions-deuros-pour-changer-dechelle/
11. **Implementation failure mode = vendor fragility, not model accuracy.** Botkeeper (~$90M raised) shut down Feb 2026 within weeks of losing 30–40% of revenue when ten large accounting-firm clients consolidated; ~600 jobs; hundreds of firms scrambled. Bench (35k SME customers) shut down 27 Dec 2024, acquired by Employer.com 3 days later; ICAEW post-mortem on lessons. r/Accounting scepticism about how much was truly autonomous vs. offshore humans. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://www.goingconcern.com/another-early-ai-accounting-startup-just-bit-the-dust/ ; https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/10/botkeeper-shutdown-ai-bookkeeping-vendor-trust-guide ; https://www.icaew.com/insights/viewpoints-on-the-news/2025/apr-2025/cloud-accounting-software-lessons-from-failure-of-bench
12. **Labour-market signal.** Indeed Q1 2026: 288 AI-involving job titles in Germany, 160 UK, 138 France; AI-skill roles carry an average 56% wage premium (up from 25%); AP/AR and large reporting teams under headcount pressure. 55% of UK FS firms plan to hire more in 2026 with AI skills in demand. — VERIFIED (search-summary), but no evidence found for specific "AI finance automation" or "Peppol integration" job postings (query returned vendor guides only). https://www.psm-paris.fr/en/ai-jobs-in-europe/ ; https://brainsource.network/resources/finance-recruitment-in-2026
13. **Pleo launched AI agents and announced layoffs one day later** (June 2026) — incumbents' own agent launches are being paired with headcount cuts, i.e. the agent story is also a cost story. — VERIFIED (search-summary). https://thenextweb.com/news/pleo-layoffs-ai-agents-finance
14. **Belgian SME AI is heavily agency/consultant-mediated** — Clutch lists dozens of Belgian AI consultancies; "Belgium's no.1 AI agency for SMEs" (LTC) sells n8n automation + ChatGPT for business + training, i.e. glue work on top of existing tools. — VERIFIED (search-summary) for existence; market size not found. https://clutch.co/be/consulting/ai
15. **Micro-SaaS reality check:** median profitable micro-SaaS ≈ $4,200 MRR; median profitable indie SaaS earns <40% of the founder's salary equivalent. — ESTIMATE (blog aggregations). https://thechrisverse.medium.com/most-business-idea-lists-are-useless-these-9-have-real-revenue-behind-them-31cd16c4f406
