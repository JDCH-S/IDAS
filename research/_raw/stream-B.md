## Regulatory status snapshot (what was delayed / softened — reduces opportunities)

- CSRD Omnibus I: FINAL (Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force 18 Mar 2026). Scope cut to >1,000 employees AND >EUR 450m turnover; wave-one companies exempt for FY2025-2026; amended rules apply from FY2027. Direct CSRD tooling for mid-caps is a shrinking market. (lw.com, consilium.europa.eu)
- CSDDD: scope narrowed to >5,000 employees and >EUR 1.5bn; first application 26 Jul 2029. Roughly 13,000 -> ~6,000 companies. (dlapiper GENIE)
- EU AI Act: high-risk obligations deferred via Digital Omnibus (in force 27 Jul 2026) to 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I). Article 50 transparency duties applied on 2 Aug 2026; Article 4 AI literacy in force since 2 Feb 2025. (gibsondunn.com, CSA labs)
- EUDR: delayed again to 30 Dec 2026 (large) / 30 Jun 2027 (micro/small); downstream operators no longer file their own DDS; simplification review due Apr 2026. (trade.ec.europa.eu, stibbe.com)
- GDPR Digital Omnibus (proposal): RoPA exemption for <750 employees; single EU DPIA list. Would cut SME documentation work. (nautadutilh.com)
- EU Pay Transparency: Belgium missed the 7 Jun 2026 transposition deadline and asked for 6 months more. (liedekerke.com)
- CBAM: Omnibus 50-tonne threshold exempts ~90% of importers. (taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu)
- NL NIS2 (Cbw): in force 15 Aug 2026 after ~2 years' delay. LU NIS2: law of 5 May 2026, ILR self-registration by 10 Jul 2026.
- Belgian mobility budget: mandatory from 1 Jan 2027 (50+ employees) / 2028 (15-49), not 2026 as first announced. (sdworx.be)
- Belgian e-invoicing: tolerance NOT extended beyond 31 Mar 2026 (self-billing to end Jun 2026); progressive fines 1,500/3,000/5,000 EUR from 1 Apr 2026. (banqup.com, vertexinc.com)

## Raw opportunities

### B-01. Peppol e-invoicing laggards in Belgium (post-tolerance clean-up)
- Problem: Mandatory B2B structured e-invoicing since 1 Jan 2026; hundreds of thousands of VAT-registered businesses were still not on Peppol at go-live and now face progressive fines.
- Who suffers: Belgian micro/small businesses invoicing from Word/Excel or legacy tools; their accountants who must onboard them.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — federal administration said in Dec 2025 that >half of companies still had steps to take; only 515,000 of 1.2m VAT-registered businesses were on Peppol (banqup). 54% of SMEs registered in the last six months; 20% after the mandatory date (itdaily). 70% of accountants said fewer than a quarter of clients were ready (ITAA survey Dec 2024). Fines EUR 1,500/3,000/5,000 from 1 Apr 2026.
- Current workaround: Accountant-led onboarding, free/cheap Peppol access points, accounting-software upgrades.
- Spend signal (WTP): Software vendors (Banqup, Accountable, Billit, Combell, e-invoice.be) actively selling; fines as a forcing function. Prices not found in summaries.
- Existing players noticed: Banqup (Unifiedpost), Accountable, Billit, EDICOM, Storecove, Combell, Peppol access points; every Belgian accounting package.
- Why now / what changed: Royal Decree 14 Jul 2025; mandate 1 Jan 2026; tolerance ended 31 Mar 2026.
- Evidence URLs: https://www.banqup.com/resources/blog/e-invoicing-grace-period-2026-belgium ; https://itdaily.com/blogs/software/peppol-smes-time-savings/ ; https://www.blogitaa.be/2024/12/12/accountants-belastingadviseurs-stomen-ondernemingen-klaar/
- Quick take: crowded — the sending/receiving layer is commoditised; only niche (e.g. non-standard invoice flows, attachments, exceptions, monitoring of rejected invoices) may be open.

### B-02. Belgian 2028 near-real-time VAT e-reporting (dual-sided) — data-quality gap
- Problem: From 1 Jan 2028 both supplier and customer must report a subset of invoice data via Peppol in near real time; it replaces the annual client listing. Companies whose Peppol data is incomplete or inconsistent will generate mismatches with the tax authority.
- Who suffers: Belgian SMEs and mid-caps with multiple invoicing systems; their accountants; ERP integrators.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — law approved (reported 23 Jul 2026, VATupdate/RTC Suite); "dual near real-time" reporting. Cost of mismatches: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: None yet; vendors say "e-reporting is not a second migration if e-invoicing was done right".
- Spend signal (WTP): E-invoicing vendors positioning; no dedicated SME reconciliation tools found.
- Existing players noticed: Storecove, Comarch, EDICOM, RTC Suite (all vendor-side).
- Why now / what changed: Law approved mid-2026, effective 1 Jan 2028.
- Evidence URLs: https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/23/belgium-approves-dual-near-real-time-vat-e-reporting-for-2028/ ; https://www.storecove.com/blog/en/mastering-belgiums-peppol-mandate-from-2026-e-invoicing-to-2028-continuous-vat-reporting/
- Quick take: promising for a finance-ops founder — a "pre-reconciliation / AR-AP matching against what the tax authority will see" layer fits FP&A/accounting skills; 2028 timing gives runway but also means slow WTP today.

### B-03. Belgian VAT-chain reform (provisierekening, 1 May 2026) causing accountant chaos
- Problem: VAT current account replaced by a provision account; filing deadlines moved; vacation regime abolished. ITAA members report filings not visible in Twinntax/MyMinfin and payment reminders for already-provisioned amounts.
- Who suffers: Belgian accounting firms (ITAA members) and their SME clients.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — ITAA blog (28 May 2026) "numerous reports" of problems. Quantified hours lost: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Manual checks on MyMinfin, calls to FPS Finance, spreadsheets.
- Spend signal (WTP): Practicali sells training on the topic; no tooling found.
- Existing players noticed: Twinntax, MyMinfin (government), accounting software vendors.
- Why now / what changed: Law modernising the VAT chain (1 Jan 2025), last phase 1 May 2026.
- Evidence URLs: https://www.blogitaa.be/2026/05/28/btw-ketting-anno-2026-invoering-van-de-btw-provisierekening-en-einde-vakantieregeling/ ; https://www.vpaccountants.be/nieuws/modernisering-btw-ketting-provisierekening-2026
- Quick take: unclear — real, acute pain but likely transient (government fixes bugs); could seed a "VAT position monitoring" feature rather than a company.

### B-04. Accountant capacity crunch in Belgium (client stops) — compliance work no one can absorb
- Problem: Accounting firms are refusing new clients because they cannot hire; at the same time regulation (e-invoicing, VAT chain, UBO, NBB filing) pushes more work to them.
- Who suffers: Belgian SMEs seeking an accountant; small accounting firms.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — 56% of surveyed firms implemented a client stop; 73% see client numbers rising with insufficient staff; 58% cite workload reduction as top priority (ITAA barometer / AccountancyVandaag). ITAA July 2026 survey of 1,642 members on software concentration/dependency.
- Current workaround: Client stops; software; offshoring; price increases.
- Spend signal (WTP): Firms invest in automation; ITAA survey shows dependence on a few software vendors.
- Existing players noticed: Exact, Yuki, Octopus, Silverfin, Wolters Kluwer, Billit; Silverfin-type automation.
- Why now / what changed: 2026 wave (e-invoicing, VAT chain, DAC8, UBO annual confirmation) hits a profession already at capacity.
- Evidence URLs: https://accountancyvandaag.be/helft-accountants-weigert-nieuwe-klanten/ ; https://www.blogitaa.be/2026/07/07/softwaremarkt-onder-druk-wat-1-642-confraters-ons-vertellen-over-concentratie-en-afhankelijkheid/
- Quick take: promising as a channel insight — accountants are the distribution point for almost every SME compliance tool below; a founder with accounting-workflow background can build for them.

### B-05. VSME "value-chain cap" — SMEs answering large-customer sustainability requests
- Problem: Large CSRD filers historically sent SME suppliers questionnaires of several hundred questions in different formats; Omnibus caps requests to VSME content, and the VSME Delegated Act (19 Jul 2026) makes VSME the de facto supplier standard from late 2026/2027.
- Who suffers: SME suppliers of large EU companies (manufacturing, logistics, construction, food); their finance/QHSE person.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — Sept-Dec 2026 "adaptation period" in which large companies start formally requesting VSME (Dcycle). Suppliers receive questionnaires from 5-20 customers simultaneously (Socialsuite). Cost per response: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Excel, copy-paste from prior questionnaires, consultants, EcoVadis.
- Spend signal (WTP): VSME software EUR 0-99/month (VSME Reporter EUR 99/month; ExecutESG EUR 49/month) up to EUR 3,000-8,000/year for platforms (ExecutESG pricing guide). Coolset, Sunhat launched VSME modules.
- Existing players noticed: Coolset, Sunhat, Dcycle, QuickVSME, VSME Reporter, ExecutESG, Zerovia, EcoVadis, Ecoactive.
- Why now / what changed: Omnibus I final (Feb 2026); VSME Delegated Act 19 Jul 2026; value-chain cap binding.
- Evidence URLs: https://dcycle.io/blog/vsme-voluntary-standard-smes-2026/ ; https://quickvsme.com/en/blog/eu-value-chain-cap-sme-suppliers ; https://executesg.com/resources/blog/esg-software-pricing-2026
- Quick take: crowded but price-sensitive; the possible opening is finance-grade VSME data (energy/GHG from accounting data) sold via accountants, not another ESG form-filler.

- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — scope from ~250 employees to >1,000 employees and >EUR 450m turnover; wave-one exemption FY2025-2026 (lw.com). Number of Belgian companies dropped: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Companies pause or downsize; vendors pivot to VSME.
- Spend signal (WTP): Falling.
- Existing players noticed: Coolset, Sunhat, Workiva, Greenomy (BE), Dcycle.
- Why now / what changed: Directive (EU) 2026/470 (in force 18 Mar 2026).

- Quick take: weak — this is a NEGATIVE signal; avoid building for mandatory CSRD reporting below the 1,000-employee line.

### B-07. NIS2 Belgium — "important entities" (~2,500) with no in-house security/compliance function
- Problem: Belgium registered ~1,500 essential and ~2,500 important entities; essential entities had to file evidence (CyFun/ISO 27001) by 18 Apr 2026; important entities must self-assess. Most "NIS2 compliance officers" are learning on the job; CAB audit capacity strained.
- Who suffers: Belgian mid-sized firms in 18 sectors (food, manufacturing, waste, chemicals, digital providers, health), often with an external IT provider and no CISO.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — ~4,000 in-scope entities (jimber/legiscope); 90 audits in 2025; first fine EUR 185,000 reported mid-2026; only 16% of businesses feel fully prepared, 84% of in-scope not ready (CyberSmart survey, 670 leaders, late 2025).
- Current workaround: Managed service providers, consultants, CyFun self-assessment tool, spreadsheets.
- Spend signal (WTP): CyFun certification ~EUR 8,000 (jimber); ISO 27001 higher; Normec accredited CAB; a large consultancy/MSP market; Secfix raised USD 12m Series A (Feb 2026) for SME security compliance.
- Existing players noticed: Secfix, Vanta, Drata, Cyberplan, Jimber, ICTLAB, Makeset, Resiliently, Easycyberprotection, Normec; CCB CyFun toolkit.
- Why now / what changed: Belgian NIS2 law in force 18 Oct 2024; registration 18 Mar 2025; evidence deadline 18 Apr 2026; enforcement starting.
- Evidence URLs: https://jimber.io/blog/nis2-fines-belgium-what-enforcement-actually-looks-like/ ; https://ccb.belgium.be/news/nis2-18-april-2026-deadline-what-essential-entities-must-have-place ; https://passwork.pro/blog/nis2-latest-news-april-2026/
- Quick take: crowded on the GRC-platform side; the underserved piece is the important-entity SME that needs a cheap evidence file + supplier attestations, sold through MSPs/accountants.

### B-08. NIS2 supply-chain cascade — non-regulated suppliers asked for security attestations
- Problem: In-scope entities must manage supply-chain security, so they push questionnaires and contractual security requirements onto suppliers not themselves in scope (NL Cbw explicitly notes chain responsibility).
- Who suffers: Small IT vendors, SaaS, logistics and facility suppliers to essential/important entities in BE/NL/LU.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — NL: ~8,000 organisations in scope; "requirements also apply to suppliers through chain responsibility"; nearly half of Dutch organisations insufficiently prepared. Number of supplier questionnaires: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Ad-hoc questionnaire answers, ISO 27001 for larger vendors.
- Spend signal (WTP): Trust-center / questionnaire-automation vendors (Vanta, Secfix) exist for SaaS; nothing found for non-tech suppliers.
- Existing players noticed: Vanta, Secfix, Drata, 3rdRisk (NL), CyFun "supplier" levels.
- Why now / what changed: BE enforcement 2026; NL Cbw 15 Aug 2026; LU law 5 May 2026.
- Evidence URLs: https://www.computable.nl/2026/07/17/nieuwe-cyberbeveiligingswet-van-kracht-dit-moet-je-weten/ ; https://luxgap.com/articles/nis2-luxembourg-transposition-mai-2026-auto-enregistrement-ilr/?lang=en
- Quick take: promising — same "supplier attestation" pattern as VSME and DORA; one shared "supplier compliance passport" for Benelux SMEs could aggregate three demand waves.

### B-09. DORA contractual cascade onto small ICT vendors of financial entities
- Problem: Financial entities must maintain a Register of Information (all ICT contracts, no materiality threshold) and impose Article 30 clauses; small software vendors receive bespoke addenda from each bank/insurer/fund and must answer sub-contractor, exit, audit-rights questions.
- Who suffers: Fintech/SaaS/IT-services SMEs selling into banks, insurers, asset managers (BE/LU/NL); also small financial entities (payment institutions, small AIFMs) building the RoI themselves.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — 46% of institutions named the RoI the single most challenging DORA requirement (Deloitte survey via regulation-dora.eu); FSMA 2026 RoI update limited in scope. Vendor-side hours: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Vendors draft a standard DORA addendum (penrose.law advice); Excel RoI templates.
- Spend signal (WTP): RegReportingDesk, 3rdRisk, Fabasoft, Sirion sell tooling; Hybridity (SE) funded for DORA/NIS2 automation.
- Existing players noticed: 3rdRisk, RegReportingDesk, Fabasoft, Sirion, Hybridity, Big4.
- Why now / what changed: DORA applies since 17 Jan 2025; annual RoI submissions (Mar 2026 limited update); Luxembourg/Belgium heavy financial-sector concentration.
- Evidence URLs: https://www.fsma.be/en/news/dora-register-information-third-party-ict-service-providers-limited-update-2026 ; https://www.regulation-dora.eu/blog/dora-register-of-information-2026-guide ; https://penrose.law/en/dora-for-it-suppliers/
- Quick take: promising niche — a vendor-side "DORA-ready pack" (standard addendum, sub-contractor chain, evidence) for Benelux/LU fintech suppliers; moderate competition, high WTP per account.

### B-10. EU AI Act Article 4 AI-literacy duty for every SME using AI tools
- Problem: Since 2 Feb 2025 every deployer must ensure sufficient AI literacy of staff; guidance says this needs a programme with evidence, not a one-off seminar. SMEs using ChatGPT/Copilot are deployers and have nothing documented.
- Who suffers: Any SME with employees using AI tools; HR/office managers; accountancy and law firms.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — obligation in force; fines up to EUR 7.5m/1% referenced; proportionality for SMEs. Enforcement cases: Evidence not found.
- Current workaround: Ignore; buy an e-learning module; policy template from a law firm.
- Spend signal (WTP): Training vendors (Compliquest, Themio, Legalithm, Delbion) selling; enterprise governance tools EUR 50k-200k/year (ovidiusuciu.com) — inaccessible to SMEs.
- Existing players noticed: Themio, Legalithm, Compliquest, Delbion, EU AI Compass, AI Assurance Institute, Deloitte Legal.
- Why now / what changed: Art. 4 applied 2 Feb 2025; Art. 50 transparency 2 Aug 2026; high-risk deferred to Dec 2027 / Aug 2028 (reduces urgency for the heavier deployer duties).
- Evidence URLs: https://www.legalithm.com/en/ai-act-guide/article-4 ; https://www.themio.ai/en/blog/best-ai-act-compliance-tools-sme-2026 ; https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/
- Quick take: weak-to-unclear — low enforcement, low WTP, many free templates; the Omnibus deferral removed the 2026 urgency for high-risk deployer work.

### B-12. DAC8 crypto reporting — CASP onboarding/self-certification and 60-day blocking

### B-14. CBAM definitive period — authorised declarant + supplier emissions data for mid-sized importers
- Problem: From 1 Jan 2026 importers of >50 t/yr of steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen must be authorised declarants, collect installation-level emissions from non-EU suppliers, report annually and buy certificates; missing data forces default values and higher cost.
- Who suffers: Belgian/EU mid-sized importers and distributors (metal trading, construction supply, machinery), customs brokers acting as indirect representatives.
- Frequency/cost signal: VERIFIED (search-summary) — application by 31 Mar 2026 to keep importing; supplier data collection "single biggest operational challenge" (coolset/zevero); ~10% of importers remain in scope covering 99% of emissions. Certificate spend: depends on data.
- Current workaround: Excel supplier templates, default values, customs brokers, Grant Thornton BE advisory.
- Spend signal (WTP): CarbonChain, Coolset, Zevero, Climease, One Click LCA, Carbon Complete; Grant Thornton BE alerts.
- Existing players noticed: CarbonChain, Coolset, Zevero, Climease, Sprih, One Click LCA; Big4.
- Why now / what changed: Definitive period 1 Jan 2026; Omnibus (Reg. 2025/2083) threshold; UK CBAM 1 Jan 2027 with no transitional phase (extra layer for BE exporters to UK / UK importers).
- Evidence URLs: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-definitive-regime_en ; https://www.grantthornton.be/en/the-field/articles-and-publications/audit/cbam-2026-why-importers-now-have-no-time-to-lose/ ; https://kpmg.com/uk/en/insights/tax/tmd-from-draft-to-delivery-hmrc-confirm-the-framework.html
- Quick take: promising for a finance person — CBAM is a cost-accounting/forecasting problem (certificate liability, provisioning, pass-through pricing) as much as a data problem; crowded on data collection, thin on the finance side.
