## First-pass findings (search batch 1, 2026-09-12)
- VERIFIED: CDP says ~45,000 suppliers were requested to disclose through its Supply Chain programme in 2025; "over 75% of large corporations now require sustainability data from their suppliers"; suppliers "receive similar questionnaires from five or ten or twenty other customers simultaneously" (vendor blog claims, not a survey). https://ditchcarbon.com/blog/stop-asking-suppliers-questions-theyve-already-answered ; https://www.assent.com/blog/esg-survey-supplier-fatigue-tips/ ; https://www.supplierspartnership.org/resources/SPSustReportingDriversOverviewMay2025.pdf
- VERIFIED (search-summary): Omnibus I Directive adopted 24 Feb 2026 introduced the legal value-chain cap: CSRD-reporting companies may not require suppliers with < 1,000 employees to provide sustainability data beyond the VSME standard; the Delegated Regulation makes the cap a legally enforceable ceiling and protected undertakings gain a statutory right to refuse requests beyond it; cap set at VSME Basic Module (~46 data points: energy, emissions, workforce, water, waste, basic governance). Commission must adopt VSME as a delegated act within 4 months of Directive entry into force. https://generationimpact.global/blog/voluntary-standard-value-chain-cap-2026/ ; https://quickvsme.com/en/blog/eu-value-chain-cap-sme-suppliers ; https://www.bclplaw.com/en-US/events-insights-news/csrd-requesting-data-from-sme-suppliers.html ; https://viewpoint.pwc.com/gx/en/pwc/in-briefs/ib_int202520.html
- VERIFIED (search-summary): CCB advises all organisations in the supply chain of a NIS2 entity to comply at least with CyFun Basic; a NIS2 entity may impose a CyFun level on direct suppliers; Port of Antwerp-Bruges requires logistics subcontractors, customs agents and transport providers to demonstrate verified maturity (typically CyFun Basic) to retain access credentials; acceptable supplier attestations: completed questionnaire, substantiated self-declaration, NIS2 Supply Chain (SC) quality mark, CyFun label, or ISO 27001. https://atwork.safeonweb.be/nis2 ; https://jimber.io/blog/nis2-supply-chain-security-obligations/ ; https://tidalcontrol.com/blog/nis2-for-suppliers-to-nis2-obligated-organizations-what-do-you-need-to-arrange-now ; https://www.cranium.eu/belgiums-first-year-of-nis2-what-cyfun-means-for-you/
- Statbel: be.STAT crosstable exists (structure of enterprises by employment size class, industry & construction, 2023 data) but could not be opened; counts to be derived from Eurostat/EC SBA below. https://bestat.statbel.fgov.be/bestat/crosstable.xhtml?view=e39fe31c-6191-44ac-ab56-022b45c7a78b

## First-pass findings (search batch 2)
- VERIFIED (search-summary) — strongest Belgian demand evidence so far: Dec 2025 survey by food-sector federations Fenavian, BFA, Brema and FVPHouse (UNIZO-affiliated): sustainability reporting demands from retailers, large food companies and financial institutions on SMEs "have completely gotten out of hand"; 96% of companies cite administrative burden, 75% increased workload; costs for software, subscriptions and consultancy "can reach tens of thousands of euros per year"; 41% must work with 3-5 different systems (portals); 75% get demands from retailers, 42% from customers, 42% from large food companies; requests often exceed legal requirement, with tight deadlines, repeated reminders and "in some cases even the threat of being removed as a supplier". https://www.unizo.be/berichten/pers/geld-dat-naar-duurzaamheid-zou-moeten-gaan-gaat-naar-rapportering ; https://www.made-in.be/oost-vlaanderen/rapporteringsdruk-rond-duurzaamheid-weegt-zwaar-op-kmos-in-voedingssector/
