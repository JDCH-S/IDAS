# Belgium founder-level facts (apply to every opportunity)

All facts below come from WebSearch result summaries (pages could not be opened). Label: VERIFIED FACT (search-summary) unless noted.

## Side business while employed ("indépendant à titre complémentaire" / "zelfstandige in bijberoep")
- An employee can register as complementary self-employed while keeping the job and its social rights.
- 2026 minimum social contribution: €98.51 per quarter; exemption possible if annual taxable income from the activity < €1,922.16; above that, 20.5% of taxable income (max contribution €5,103.05).
- Sources: https://www.accountable.eu/fr-be/blog/cotisations-sociales-independant-complementaire/ ; https://www.secretariat-social-belgique.com/guides/independant-complementaire/ ; http://info.hub.brussels/en/guide/start-business-formalities/self-employed-social-status-obligations-and-rights

## VAT
- Domestic small-business VAT exemption ("franchise") up to €25,000 annual turnover; no VAT charged, no input VAT recovery; voluntary registration possible. Threshold rises toward €30,000 by 2031 (vatcalc). Cross-border SME scheme since 2025: EU-wide €100,000 threshold plus the other member state's national threshold.
- Sources: https://finance.belgium.be/en/enterprises/vat/vat-obligation/vat-exemption-scheme-small-businesses ; https://www.bdo.be/en-gb/insights/news-alerts/2024/6-key-details-vat-franchise-regime-for-small-businesses-in-belgium-starting-2025 ; https://www.vatcalc.com/belgium/belgium-raises-vat-registration-threshold-to-e30000/
- Note: B2B e-invoicing mandate (structured e-invoices via Peppol) applies to VAT-registered businesses for domestic B2B since 1 Jan 2026; businesses under the franchise regime are commonly reported as out of scope for issuing (to confirm). Penalties €1,500/€3,000/€5,000. 120% cost deduction for small companies. Sources: https://www.vertexinc.com/resources/resource-library/belgiums-2026-e-invoicing-regulations-explained-scope-deadlines-and-penalties ; https://edicomgroup.com/blog/belgium-will-make-b2b-electronic-invoice-mandatory

## Legal form
- Sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak / entreprise individuelle): registration ≈ €109 + VAT activation ≈ €60-80. No capital.
- BV/SRL: no minimum capital since 2019 but "sufficient equity" + financial plan; notarial deed required; all-in 2026 cost ≈ €1,500-2,500 (notary €750-1,500, CBE ≈ €90, Moniteur ≈ €200). Corporate tax 25%, reduced 20% on first €100k for small companies. Accountants commonly advise switching to a company around €80k turnover.
- Sources: https://avocat20min.be/en/blog/creer-srl-belgique-etapes-capital-frais-2026 ; https://www.accountable.eu/en-be/blog/sole-trader-to-company-when-how/ ; https://www.cleartax.com/be/company-registration-in-belgium
- Implication: validate as sole proprietor under the VAT franchise (< €300 to start), incorporate only after traction.

## Employment-law check (flag for founder)
- Post-termination non-compete clauses: valid only in writing, similar activities, limited to Belgian territory, max 12 months, salary > €88,895 (2026) or CBA-authorized above €44,447.
- During employment: general duty of loyalty and prohibition on unfair competition applies. Search did not surface a statutory requirement for employer permission for an unrelated side activity; the employment contract and work rules must be checked. Evidence not found for a general permission requirement.
- Sources: https://www.pwclegal.be/en/news/non-compete-clauses-in-belgian-labour-law--a-quickstart-guide-.html ; https://leglobal.law/countries/belgium/employment-law/employment-law-overview-belgium/08-restrictive-covenants/

## Subsidies that let SME customers pay for a founder's consulting/training (distribution lever)
- Flanders KMO-portefeuille: 30% subsidy (max €7,500/yr) small, 20% medium; 45%/35% for 'digitalization – cybersecurity' and 'sustainability – energy'. Since 1 Feb 2026 consulting subsidy limited to the 'digitalization – cybersecurity' theme; training remains open for all themes incl. AI. Provider must be registered. Sources: https://www.vlaio.be/en/node/294 ; https://www.kern-it.be/en/definitions/sme-wallet/
- Wallonia chèques-entreprises: digital-maturity voucher covers 50% of consulting fees up to €50,000 per beneficiary over 3 years; reform adopted May 2026, some aid up to 90%. Sources: https://www.cheques-entreprises.be/ ; https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/politique-belge/2026/05/21/la-reforme-des-cheques-entreprises-definitivement-validee-par-le-gouvernement-wallon-L7KPIUYK5FAZPP3TH5DVRDNZNE/ ; https://ia-pme.be/articles/cheques-entreprises-financer-projet-ia-2026
- Brussels consultancy subsidy: 60% reimbursement, net premium €300-7,500, but temporarily suspended from 12 Aug 2026 (search-summary). Source: https://economy-employment.brussels/news-new-subsidies-SME-2024 ; https://info.hub.brussels/en/guide/subsidies-entrepreneurs/economic-transition-consultancy-grant
- Implication: a service-first phase can be partly subsidized for Flemish/Walloon customers if the founder registers as a service provider (registration effort and eligibility to verify).
